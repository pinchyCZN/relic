PCDEMOS RELIC                                              Analysis Note


                       THE CITY, SEEN FROM ABOVE


Abstract

   The demo's largest shot: a city photographed from overhead with a
   fire already burning in it, then a blast that opens across the
   picture.

   IT IS NOT A SEPARATE ROUTINE. It is a later loop of
   Valley_RunScene, reached after the valley's impact, with a
   different picture and a different height map swapped in. It gets
   its own note and its own part because it is a different shot,
   not because the demo separates it.

   THE GEOMETRY IS NOT GEOMETRY. The city is a PHOTOGRAPH - a
   palettised still out of relic.dat - and what looks like it being
   destroyed is the same height-map threshold the valley's scorch
   uses, eating outward across the picture.

1.  Where It Sits

   Valley_RunScene 0x00314304 runs SIX loops, not the four
   effects/valley.txt 1 lists, and the last three are the city
   [verified]:

       +---+------------+------+--------+---------------------------+
       | # | loop       | rate | frames | picture                   |
       +===+============+======+========+===========================+
       | 1 | 0x00314402 |    - | a cue  | the valley                |
       | 2 | 0x00314438 |    - | size   | the valley                |
       | 3 | 0x003144c2 |    1 |  0x122 | the valley                |
       | 4 | 0x0031460c |    3 |   0xe6 | Farmdata, as loaded       |
       | 5 | 0x003146f2 |    4 |   0x96 | baked, 160 x 100          |
       | 6 | 0x0031482e |    3 | a cue  | Farmdata, MIRRORED        |
       +---+------------+------+--------+---------------------------+

   Between 5 and 6 the frame is filled with 0x3f3f003f and
   presented three times - a WHITE FLASH cutting the two apart.

   Loop 5 is the only one of the three whose picture is frame
   sized. 0x003146a8 decodes 0x3e80 texels - 16000, which is 160 by
   100 exactly - where loops 4 and 6 decode 0x5fa0, which is 160 by
   153 and taller than the frame.

   AND ALL THREE DECODE INTO THE SAME TWO BUFFERS, the picture into
   (0x0033b0ca) and the 0x80-colour layer into (0x0033b0c6)
   [verified, 0x00314692 and 0x003146ae]. So loop 5's 0x3e80 leaves
   ROWS 100 TO 152 HOLDING LOOP 4's PICTURE, and City_BlitBase puts
   no ceiling on its row - the source row is y plus the pan - so
   once loop 5's pan is past row 0 those rows are read and drawn.
   Loop 5's bottom edge is the previous shot's photograph, not slack.

   The height map and the mask are NOT decoded into a buffer: loop 5
   points straight at 0x00335fe0 and 0x00339e60 in the executable, so
   reading past 153 rows of those reads whatever the image holds
   next, and the reconstruction gets it for free by pointing at the
   same bytes.

2.  Shots One And Three Are One Photograph

   Loop 6 does not load anything new. It re-decodes the SAME two
   Farmdata parts loop 4 used and then flips all three of its
   assets in place [verified]:

       0x00314790   Stills_MirrorPixels on the 0x80-palette layer
       0x0031479a   Stills_MirrorPixels on the main picture
       0x003147a4   Stills_MirrorBytes  on the height map

   Stills_MirrorPixels 0x003148d4 walks 0x98 + 1 = 153 rows and
   swaps 0x4f + 1 = 80 pairs a row, pixel i against pixel 159 - i.
   THAT 153 IS FARMDATA'S PICTURE HEIGHT, not the frame's 100, so
   it flips the source still rather than anything drawn.
   Stills_MirrorBytes 0x003148b0 is the same walk at a 0xa0 stride
   for the byte map, so the destruction spreads to match.

   So the first and third shots are ONE PHOTOGRAPH SEEN BOTH WAYS -
   looking left above the city, then looking right - and the demo
   pays for one still rather than two.

   Loop 6 also swaps the palette: 0x0033a634 where loop 4 used
   0x0033aa34, so the same picture burns in a different colour.

3.  The Assets

   FARMDATA IS SPLIT FOUR WAYS AND THE PARTS ARE NOT THE SAME SIZE.
   g_FarmdataSplit 0x00316714 holds a count of 4 and four pointers;
   their live addresses give the lengths, and each matches its role
   exactly [observed]:

       +0x00000   0x63a0   0x400 palette + 24480 indices, the city
       +0x063a0   0x61a0   0x200 palette + 24480 indices, scorched
       +0x0c540   0x0bf4   3060 = 20 bytes x 153 rows, THE MASK
       +0x0d134   0x5fa0   24480 = 160 x 153, the height map

   Part 3's 3060 is 20 bytes a row over 153 rows, which is the
   1bpp mask City_BaseAndSpawn walks at 0x14 bytes a row - so the
   split's own arithmetic confirms section 4's reading.

   THE CITY PICTURES ARE PALETTISED, unlike the valley's. 0x0031429a
   decodes them: a palette of D0 longwords first, then one INDEX
   BYTE a texel, each index resolved through the palette and put
   through the same b0.b1.00.b2 rotate masked 0xfcfcfcfc and shifted
   down two that every other picture path in this module uses.

   Loops 4 and 6 pass D0 = 0x100 and D7 = 0x5fa0 - 256 palette
   entries, 0x400 bytes, and 24480 texels, which is 0x63a0 together
   and matches the part size exactly. 24480 IS 160 BY 153, taller
   than the frame.

   Loop 5 is different in every respect. Its picture is BAKED IN THE
   EXECUTABLE rather than in relic.dat - 0x00326194 through
   Valley_LoadDeepImage and 0x00331d60 through the palettised
   decoder at 0x3e80 texels - and its maps are baked too, 0x00335fe0
   and 0x00339e60.

4.  The Picture Becomes The Particles

   THE TWO ROUTINES ALL THREE SHOTS CALL ARE ONE MECHANISM.
   City_BaseAndSpawn 0x00315c34 draws the base and spawns, and
   City_DrawParticles 0x0031589e plots what it spawned [verified].
   The loop runs base, particles, haze, present - IN THAT ORDER
   [verified, 0x00314652 to 0x00314664], so the haze lies over the
   particles rather than being punched through by them.

   Per column, with D4 the height map byte less the threshold:

       D4 <  0        frame = the picture, untouched
       D4 >= 0        frame = splatTable[D4] + PART 2

   SO PART 2 IS WHAT IS UNDERNEATH. The intact city is part 1 and
   the scorched ground is part 2, and the burn crossfades one into
   the other as the threshold falls. Nothing blends them - a texel
   is either one or the other, with the splat colour added on top
   of the second [verified, 0x00315d7a against 0x00315cb8]. The
   add is adda.l, a plain 32-bit add with no per-byte clamp, but
   neither operand's bytes can carry so the distinction never
   shows.

   A texel spawns when D4 - 0x12 falls inside the band, the count
   is under 0x3a98 - 15000 - and the mask bit is set.

   g_CitySpawnBand is the cut's change THIS FRAME: 0x00315da0
   holds the previous frame's threshold >> 2 and 0x00315c90
   subtracts the new one, so only the newly exposed strip spawns.
   Each shot's entry seeds it at 0xff, which makes the first frame
   of a shot spawn across the whole already-burnt area at once.

   THE MASK IS A BIT STREAM OVER CANDIDATES, NOT A STENCIL. One
   longword is loaded per group of 0x20 columns, but the roxl.l
   that shifts a bit out sits AFTER both height tests, so a column
   that fails the height consumes nothing. Within a group the first
   candidate takes bit 31, the next bit 30, and so on. A particle's
   chance therefore has nothing to do with the mask's shape at that
   pixel [verified, 0x00315cc4 to 0x00315cd8].

   THE PARTICLE TAKES THE PICTURE'S OWN PIXEL AS ITS COLOUR. D3 is
   the longword just read out of the picture and goes straight to
   the node's +0x20. The photograph is not destroyed - IT IS TAKEN
   APART INTO ITS OWN PIXELS, which is what reads as the city
   coming down.

   The particles are a DOUBLY LINKED LIST. Nodes are unlinked from
   g_CityParticleFree and linked into g_CityParticleHead, whose
   successor slot doubles as the walk's sentinel. The node:

       +0x00  next            +0x04  prev
       +0x08  x, 24.8         +0x0c  y, 24.8
       +0x16  size selector   +0x18  vx, 24.8
       +0x1c  vy, 24.8        +0x20  colour

   X AND Y ARE 24.8, not 16.16 - both are read with asr.l #8
   [verified, 0x003158c8]. The y is stored with the scroll already
   added and the draw subtracts the current scroll, so the pan
   carries a particle with the picture it came from.

   A PARTICLE HAS NO RADIUS, ONLY A SHAPE. 0x00315900 is
   jmp ([0x3159ce], D5w * 4) with the selector HALVED, and each of
   the eight arms writes a different footprint:

       +-----------+-------+-----------------------------------+
       | arm       | texels| footprint                         |
       +===========+=======+===================================+
       | 0x315908  |     4 | a 2 x 2 block                     |
       | 0x31591c  |     3 | an L                              |
       | 0x31592c  |     2 | a pair across                     |
       | 0x315936  |     1 | one                               |
       | 0x31593c  |     3 | the other L                       |
       | 0x31594c  |     2 | a diagonal pair                   |
       | 0x315958  |     2 | the same                          |
       | 0x315964  |     1 | one                               |
       +-----------+-------+-----------------------------------+

   The 0x3c table entries are NOT ordered by size - they map onto
   those eight in a scrambled order, so a particle's shape jumps
   about as it ages rather than shrinking cleanly [verified].

   AND THE SAME FIELD IS THE LIFETIME. 0x00315986 takes one off
   +0x16 every draw and the node dies when it goes negative. The
   selector comes from *g_pCitySizeTable at a cursor that advances
   a BYTE a spawn, so it wraps at 256; 0x003142bc fills that table
   with (Valley_Rand(0x2c) + 0x10) * 2, an even 0x20 to 0x76, which
   is a life of 32 to 118 draws.

   The step is in City_DrawParticles too, after the plot:

       x += vx      vx -= vx >> 3      drag, 7/8 a draw
       y += vy      vy untouched       no gravity

   Every clip failure jumps to the same exit as death, so an
   off-screen particle is recycled at once - unlinked from the
   active list, pushed onto g_CityParticleFree and the count
   decremented. The clip is x >= 0x9f and y >= 0x62.

4.1.  Each Shot Throws The City A Different Way

   THE VELOCITY TABLE IS REBUILT AT EVERY TRANSITION, one pair a
   texel, and there is a different builder for each shot
   [verified]:

       +------+------------+-------------------------------------+
       | shot | builder    | origin                              |
       +======+============+=====================================+
       | 1    | 0x0031571a | dx = col - 0xa0, off the RIGHT edge |
       | 2    | 0x003156b6 | the frame's centre                  |
       | 3    | 0x00315796 | dx = col, off the LEFT edge         |
       +------+------------+-------------------------------------+

   Shots one and three share a template and differ only in that
   first constant, which mirrors the throw the way 0x003148d4
   mirrors the picture. Both put the origin 0x14 rows ABOVE the
   picture's top and compute

       d  = isqrt(dx * dx + dy * dy)        dy = row + 0x14
       vx = (dx * d) >> 5 + Valley_Rand(0x64) - 0x32
       vy = (dy * d) >> 6 + Valley_Rand(0x64) - 0x32

   THE SPEED GROWS WITH THE DISTANCE - dx * d, not dx / d - so the
   far side of the city is thrown hardest, and vy's extra shift
   makes the throw twice as wide as it is deep. They fill the
   PICTURE's 153 rows; shot two fills the frame's 100 and uses a
   flat gradient from the centre instead:

       vx = (col - 0x50) * 4 + Valley_Rand(0xc8) - 0x64
       vy = (row - 0x32) * 2 + Valley_Rand(0x64) - 0x32

   The table is NOT scrolled when it is read - A5 is loaded
   straight from 0x0033b0ba - so a texel's velocity goes with its
   place on the screen, not its place in the photograph.

4.2.  The Pan Is Bounded By The Picture

   The scroll is 0x0033b03c, latched to 0x0033b038 each frame, and
   City_BaseAndSpawn takes it >> 8 as a row offset into the picture,
   the layer and the map.

   THE CAP IS THE PICTURE'S SLACK. The hook stops it at 0x3500,
   which is 53 rows, and the picture is 153 tall against a 100 row
   frame - exactly 53 of slack [verified]. So the pan runs the
   full height of the still and halts at its bottom edge.

   EACH SHOT STAGES THE PAN DIFFERENTLY [verified]:

       +------+------------+--------+---------------------------+
       | shot | staged at  | vel    | scroll                    |
       +======+============+========+===========================+
       |  1   | 0x00314602 | -0x64  | inherited                 |
       |  2   | nowhere    | -      | latch cleared every frame |
       |  3   | 0x003147e8 |  0x32  | cleared                   |
       +------+------------+--------+---------------------------+

   SHOT THREE OPENS ALREADY MOVING. Starting it from rest instead
   desynchronises the pan from the burn - both run off the same
   per-field hook, so a pan that lags leaves more of the city burnt
   at any given height, and by the time a building reaches a given
   row the sweep has gone further than it should.

   NOTHING HERE IS WALL-CLOCK OR CPU BOUND. The pan at 0x00316444,
   the threshold at 0x00316476 and the shot's own frame count at
   0x00237218 are all stepped by the VBL, so their ratios are fixed
   whatever the frame rate. Only how many DRAWS fall inside those
   fields varies with load, and that changes smoothness, not the
   relationship between the pan and the burn.

   IT ACCELERATES, AND IT STARTS BY NOT MOVING AT ALL. 0x0033b040
   is a velocity that grows 2 a field up to 0x190, and the scroll
   advances by that >> 2 - but 0x00316458 branches away on a
   NEGATIVE shift, so while the velocity is below zero it only
   climbs and the shot holds still [verified].

   The velocity is staged in the PREAMBLE, not in the shot's own
   block: 0x0031460c writes neither 0x0033b03c nor 0x0033b040, but
   0x00314602 puts the velocity to -0x64 on the way in, alongside
   the 0x003145fa that starts the haze's drift. So the pan is
   stationary for its first 50 fields and only then eases away
   [verified].

4.3.  The Reconstruction

   src/part_city.c, part 2.18, with the machinery in src/stills.c.

   THE PART BOUNDARY IS OURS. The demo flows out of the valley's
   impact into these loops without re-entering, so part_city.c
   stages counters the valley's loops would have left behind, and
   shot three's length is ours too - the demo ends it on a cue.

   THE SHOT LENGTHS ARE FIELDS TOO. 0x00237218 is stepped by
   Vbl_Server, so 0xe6 and 0x96 are field counts and a shot that
   counts its own drawn frames overruns - see valley.txt 2.11.

   EVERY COUNTER STEPS ONCE A FIELD, not once a draw. A reading 15
   fields into shot one, run 160 fields on, has the demo's
   threshold, scroll and pan velocity match our tick 176 exactly -
   275, 3906 and 252 [observed]. The demo draws more slowly than
   it steps, but the band is the cut's change per DRAW, so a
   slower frame rate spawns more per frame and the same texels
   spawn in total.

   Scored against hardware, raw to raw, both references taken
   off the TOP of the shot's loop so the frame is finished - see
   valley.txt 2.4:

       shot one, 173 fields on     R 5.59  G 4.09  LIGHT 0.08  B 3.81
       shot two,  34 fields on     R 0.82  G 0.62  LIGHT 0.40  B 0.61
       shot three, 30 fields on    R 0.54  G 0.40  LIGHT 0.03  B 0.34

   Shot three's scroll and pan velocity match hardware exactly,
   585 and 110.

   SHOT THREE RUNS 212 FIELDS. Its loop ends on the music, not a
   count - 0x00314858 calls 0x0021ff64 and loops while it answers
   zero - so the length has to be measured rather than read. On the
   field it exits, 0x0031666a clears 0x00237218 from 0xd4, and the
   other two counters agree: the threshold is 0x84, which is 0x300
   less three a field for 212 fields, and the scroll is 0x3490 with
   the pan velocity capped [observed].

   THE PAN FINISHES AS THE SHOT CUTS. 0x3490 is 112 short of the
   0x3500 cap and the capped velocity moves 100 a field, so the
   still is panned to its bottom edge within a field of the music
   ending the scene. The BURN does not finish - 0x84 of the
   threshold is left - which is what leaves unburnt buildings in a
   corner at the cut.

   THE RECONSTRUCTION HOLDS THE TRANSITIONS. part_city.c draws each
   of the first two shots for its loop's field count and then stops
   drawing for the balance, so the next shot starts on the field the
   demo starts it on and shot three gets its full length. The
   unburnt parts match to within a level a channel [observed], and
   the residue is the particle field, whose placement follows a
   generator we do not reproduce - which is why the wide first shot
   scores worse than the second.

   EVERY SHOT ENTRY EMPTIES THE PARTICLE POOL. City_ResetParticles
   0x00315ace re-links all 0x3a98 nodes into the free list and
   zeroes the count, and all four entries call it - 0x00314360,
   0x003145c8, 0x0031468e and 0x003147ac. Skip it and the previous
   shot's debris keeps falling through the next one [verified].

   SHOT TWO HAZES EITHER SIDE OF ITS PARTICLES. Its loop is base,
   haze on the DOUBLED threshold, particles, haze on the straight
   one, present - so one pass lies under the debris and one over
   it. The other two shots haze once, after the particles.

   THE ROTATE'S LOW BYTE IS THE SOURCE'S LOWEST, NOT ITS SECOND,
   and this is the one place in the module where getting it wrong
   is invisible until a colour is examined. lsl.l #8 leaves
   B2.B1.B0.00 and lsr.w #8 acts on the LOW WORD, where B0 sits in
   the high half, so the result is B2.B1.00.B0 - the last byte is
   v & 0xff.

   Reading it as (v >> 8) & 0xff picks up B1 instead. For a palette
   entry laid out 00.RR.GG.BB that puts GREEN where BLUE belongs
   and every burnt texel comes out with G equal to B - a pink burn
   where the demo's is orange, with the error invisible in any grey
   part of the picture [observed].

   Shot two's still, map and mask are all baked in the executable
   rather than loaded, and shot three mirrors the picture, the
   layer and the map but NOT the mask [verified, 0x0031479e].

5.  What Is Known

   Shot one shows the city from overhead, skyscrapers in grey and
   white, with a small orange fire burning around x 115 to 135, y 5
   to 20. ITS LIGHT PLANE IS ENTIRELY ZERO [observed], so nothing
   had begun when it was taken.

   The machinery is the valley's, which is why the shot should come
   cheaply once the valley is finished:

       Valley_ImpactBase   thresholds a height map, halves the
                           picture under it and adds a palette
                           colour - effects/valley.txt 2.9
       0x00316476          takes 0x0033b050 off the threshold every
                           field, which is what makes the lens
                           spread
       Valley_DrawHaze     a perspective plasma on LIGHT, gated on
                           the same map - effects/valley.txt 2.10

5.1.  Each Shot's Length, Against The Music

   THE RATE 0x0033b050 DATES EVERY SHOT WITHOUT DISTORTING ONE. It is
   written once a shot and nowhere else in the scene - 3, 4 and 3, at
   0x0031461e, 0x003146d6 and 0x0031480c - so a single logging watch
   over a clean run gives all three entries with the field each
   landed on. Counted from the (0x25,0x1d) that starts the valley's
   last loop [observed, normal speed]:

       +--------+-------+--------+--------+----------------------+
       | shot   | field | fields | loop   | the balance          |
       +========+=======+========+========+======================+
       | one    |   332 |   231  |  0xe6  | 1, the decode        |
       | two    |   563 |   159  |  0x96  | 9, white and decode  |
       | three  |   722 |   212  | a cue  | -                    |
       | ends   |   934 |   602  |   -    | -                    |
       +--------+-------+--------+--------+----------------------+

   THE SCENE IS 602 FIELDS AND ITS END IS THE CUE'S: 934 fields after
   (0x25,0x1d) against the 935 the demo's own cue-to-cue span puts
   between that pair and (0x26,0x2e). Ours runs 601, with shot three
   measuring 210 against the demo's 212 - a field span and a musical
   span are the same thing here, as they are everywhere: the demo
   drops no fields - reconstruction.txt 4.5. This passage of Cmodule2
   runs slow, about ten fields a row, so a field of disagreement
   costs a tenth of a row.

   A SHOT IS LONGER THAN ITS LOOP AND THE DIFFERENCE IS NOT DRAWN.
   What follows a loop is a splat table, a velocity table of 24480
   entries and two pictures decoded, and the demo's last frame stands
   through all of it.

   THE WHITE IS THREE OF SHOT TWO'S NINE. 0x00314740 fills the work
   buffer and 0x0031474a presents it three times, on consecutive
   fields because there is nothing left to render; the six that
   follow present nothing at all, so the white stands through the
   decode as well [observed].

   LANDMINE: A BREAKPOINT ON EACH THRESHOLD SITE GIVES 243, 195 AND
   132, which sum to a plausible 570. The error is not in the sum but
   in its split - the halts cost the first two shots nothing and the
   last one everything - and the counters at the cut are what catch
   it: 0x00237218 reads 0xd4 where a 132-field shot would leave 0x84.
   Date the shots off a logging watch, never a halt.

   LANDMINE: MEASURING THIS SCENE UNDER WARP REDISTRIBUTES IT RATHER
   THAN SHIFTING IT, which is why a warp sweep is not a cheap version
   of this one. Warp starves the scene while the music keeps absolute
   time, so each threshold is reached five to six rows further into
   the score - 25.3f, 26.15 and 26.28 - while the cue that ends the
   scene stays on (0x26,0x2e). The slack all comes out of the last
   shot: warp put shot three at 67 fields against the 212 it really
   runs, and left the first two looking right.

   SHOT THREE IS THE ONE THAT SHOWS, because the scene ends on its
   cue. Every field the first two shots take beyond their own is one
   it loses, and at 67 it was barely half way through its burn when
   the music ended it.

   NEITHER BURN FINISHES. Shot two's threshold of 672 at 4 a field
   wants 168 fields and its loop runs 150; shot three's 768 at 3 a
   field wants 256 and gets 212. The white cuts one off mid-burn and
   the cue the other.


5.2.  The Blank Between The City And The Planet

   THE SCENE'S EXIT BLANKS THE DISPLAY. 0x00314866 writes
   g_CopperBlankStills into COP1LC and strobes COPJMP1, and that list
   is three longwords - BPLCON0 = 0x0000, COLOR00 = 0x000, end - so
   no bitplane is fetched and the screen is the background colour
   [verified]. Nothing of the city is on screen from that field on;
   its last presented frame is the one its own cue lands on.

   EVERY MODULE CARRIES ITS OWN COPY of that list, byte for byte -
   g_CopperBlank 0x002c8228, g_CopperBlank2 0x0034546c and
   g_CopperBlankTemple 0x00347674 are the same three longwords
   [verified]. Each is an immediate in the instruction that installs
   it, so none of them carries an xref. The temple module installs
   its copy twice, and effects/temple.txt 11.9 has those.

   IT LASTS 67 FIELDS, which is the span to the next copper list,
   installed by Space_DisplayOn 0x002d542e. The scene's first frame
   is presented three fields after that. The 67 split in two
   [observed]:

       +--------+----------------------------------------------------+
       | fields | what is happening                                  |
       +========+====================================================+
       |   60   | Space_RunDestruction waiting on (0x26,0x34), armed |
       |        | at 0x002c8448 the field after the city exits and   |
       |        | waited on at 0x002c844e                            |
       |    7   | its tables, built once the wait returns            |
       +--------+----------------------------------------------------+

   So sixty of them are the music's and seven are the machine's.

   THE RECONSTRUCTION SPLITS IT WHERE THE PARTS SPLIT. part_city.c
   paints black until (0x26,0x34) and part 2.19 opens with seven
   fields of black before its own counters start - effects/
   destruction.txt 2.


6.  Open questions

   -  THE BURNT REGION'S COLOUR, LATE IN A SHOT, and the error grows
      with the burn. Thirty fields into shot three the frame scores
      R 0.54; at the cut, 212 fields in, it scores R 23.78 G 19.31
      LIGHT 4.12 B 13.53 against the same field of the demo's
      [observed]. Our heavily burnt area reads mid-tone orange where
      the demo's goes near black - 2976 pixels under R 30 against
      the demo's 4961 - and shifting the frame a row either way
      barely moves the score, so it is tonal and not an alignment.
      It is a splat-and-layer question more than a particle one:
      g_CityParticleCount at the cut reads 11123 against our 9943.
