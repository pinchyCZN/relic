PCDEMOS RELIC                                              Analysis Note


                  THE VALLEY, THE BIRDS AND THE ROCK


Abstract

   Stills_RunModule's one scene: a green forested valley under a red
   sky, opening on a flock of pixel-sized birds and ending with a
   rock coming down. It follows the return module of
   effects/debris.txt and is the fourth of act two's six calls.

   It is unlike every scene before it in one respect - ITS PICTURE
   IS A PHOTOGRAPH EMBEDDED IN THE EXECUTABLE, and the frame is
   repainted from it every pass rather than accumulated.

1.  Where It Sits

   Stills_RunModule 0x00313f6c is three calls and the middle one is
   the whole scene [verified]:

       0x00313fa8   setup
       0x00314304   Valley_RunScene
       0x00313f7c   teardown

   Valley_RunScene sets the light base to 0x7f/0x5f/0x4f, hooks the
   VBL at Valley_VblHook 0x003163c0, stages a long list of globals
   and then runs SIX LOOPS, not one. The first three are this
   scene and the last three are the city - effects/city.txt 1
   [verified]:

       0x00314402   the birds alone      - section 2
       0x00314438   the rock falling     - section 2.5
       0x003144c2   the impact           - section 2.9
       0x0031460c   the city, shot one   - effects/city.txt
       0x003146f2   the city, shot two   - effects/city.txt
       0x0031482e   the city, shot three - effects/city.txt

   THE LOOPS ARE EASY TO CONFUSE AND ONE GLOBAL SEPARATES THEM.
   They all clear 0x00237218 and set 0x0033b044, so the frame
   counter cannot tell them apart. g_ValleyPhaseRate 0x0033b050
   can: the impact sets 1, the city's first and third shots 3 and
   its second 4. Only the impact draws the blast, the base pass
   and the trail; the city's loops draw 0x00315c34, 0x0031589e and
   Valley_DrawHaze.

   The second ends when the rock's size falls under 0x1400 and the
   third begins by clearing 0x0033b04e, which stops the stepper,
   and setting 0x0033b044, which stops the rock easing.

   THE GROUND IS A GREEN FORESTED VALLEY, not dunes or sand - the
   picture of section 2.1 is a photograph of wooded hills under a
   red sky, with a stony foreground [observed, on hardware].

2.  The Frame

   Four calls, in this order [verified]:

       Valley_BlitBase    0x00315b42   the picture, 16000 longwords
       Valley_SkyFlash    0x00314882   gated; the impact's flash
       Valley_DrawBirds   0x003148f8   eight one-pixel birds
       Valley_PostPass    0x00315f6a   the blur at 0x0031636c

   EVERY FRAME REPAINTS FROM THE PICTURE, so nothing accumulates in
   the colour plane.

   THE OPENING USES NO LIGHT AND THE REST OF THE SCENE USES A GREAT
   DEAL. The light plane is entirely zero while the birds have it to
   themselves, because the picture and the birds both write zero
   there; the rock and the impact then drive it past 0xfe, well
   outside the 0..0x3f the space scenes keep to [observed].

   A FRAME HERE IS READ FROM *(0x0033b0b2), NOT 0x0033b0ae. The
   blur takes 0x0033b0ae as its source and writes the other, and
   Valley_PostPass resolves the other, so 0x0033b0ae is the work
   buffer mid-draw. A capture lands between Valley_BlitBase and the
   rock, where the work buffer holds nothing but the bare picture.

2.1.  The Picture

   An IFF DEEP FORM sits in the executable at 0x00316748, 0xbbc4
   bytes, its DGBL chunk giving 0xa0 by 0x64 - 160 by 100, the
   frame's own size [verified]. It is NOT in relic.dat, which is
   why no chunk name describes this scene.

   Valley_LoadDeepImage 0x0031426a decodes it once into
   *(0x0033b0ca), and 0x00316738 then points at the decoded copy
   that Valley_BlitBase reads.

2.2.  The Birds

   Eight of them, each ONE PIXEL, from a table written into the
   code rather than into data - x, y and a phase offset
   [verified]:

       +----------+----------+---------+
       |    x     |    y     |  phase  |
       +==========+==========+=========+
       | 90       | 70       | 0       |
       | 92       | 72       | 0x3e8   |
       | 87       | 75       | 0x5dc   |
       | 95       | 77       | 0x6a4   |
       | 85       | 72       | 0x320   |
       | 80       | 72       | 0x384   |
       | 55       | 72       | 0x320   |
       | 57       | 72       | 0x384   |
       +----------+----------+---------+

   Valley_DrawBird 0x003149f8 takes three sine pairs at three phase
   globals plus the bird's own offset, each masked 0x1fff, and
   displaces the bird by their sum >> 8 >> 4. The second table is
   g_SinTable 0xc00 on from the first.

   THE FIRST PAIR IS DEAD. 0x00314a0c loads it and 0x00314a26
   overwrites both registers before either is read, so only the
   second and third phases move a bird.

   The colour alternates on BIT 8 of the first phase plus the
   bird's offset - 0x28280030 against 0x08080010 - and that is the
   wing beat. Both have a zero LIGHT byte.

   x is clipped at both ends; Y IS CLIPPED ONLY BELOW, so a bird
   past the bottom indexes straight past the frame.

   The phases and the drift are stepped in Valley_DrawBirds, ONCE
   PER DRAW rather than per field:

       0x00314a8e += 0x1c3        0x00314a92 += 0x53
       0x00314a96 += 0x1f         0x00314a9a += 1

   and the drift wraps from 0x64 back to -0x64. That is the flock
   crossing the screen a pixel a draw and starting over.

2.3.  The Sky Flash Is Lightning

   Valley_SkyFlash 0x00314882 is gated on 0x0033b04c, and THAT
   COUNTER IS RANDOM LIGHTNING RATHER THAN THE IMPACT. Valley_
   StepScene counts it down while it is positive, and at zero a
   Valley_Rand(0x50) of zero - one field in eighty - re-arms it
   with Valley_Rand(0xa) plus one [verified]. So the band flickers
   for one to ten fields at a time throughout the scene, from the
   opening onward.

   It adds 0x20100000 - R plus 0x20, G plus 0x10, nothing to LIGHT
   or B - to every longword of a row, taking 0x02010000 off the
   addend after each one.

   IT ENDS ON dble, NOT dbf. The condition codes come from the
   subi.l at 0x003148a4 and the condition is LE, so the loop stops
   the moment the addend reaches zero - SIXTEEN ROWS IN, never
   seventeen. The 0x1d counter is an upper bound that is never
   reached, and the addend never goes negative.

   Measured on the demo against a quiet baseline, with six fields
   of flash accumulated through the blur: R +26 and G +12 at row
   0, tapering to R +1 at row 15 and EXACTLY ZERO FROM ROW 16
   DOWN, with B untouched throughout [observed].

   Reading the 0x1d as a plain dbf runs the full band, drives the
   addend negative and takes red and green out of rows 17 to 29.
   That leaves a blue wash sweeping down the screen which the demo
   never shows.

2.4.  The Blur

   Valley_BlurFrame 0x0031636c averages the pixel above and below,
   then left and right, over 0x3e7f longwords with a 0x280 row
   stride - 160 by 100 four-byte pixels.

   Every add is masked 0xfefefefe before roxr.l #1, which halves
   four bytes inside one longword without carrying between them.

   IT READS 0x0033b0ae AND WRITES 0x0033b0b2, which is also what
   gets presented. SO THE FINISHED FRAME IS NEVER THE ONE THE REST
   OF THE MODULE DRAWS INTO: 0x0033b0ae holds a render in progress
   for most of a loop iteration, and a capture taken from it, or
   taken on no particular instruction, catches a part-drawn frame
   that still reads as a plausible image.

   To capture a scored reference, stop on the TOP of the scene's
   loop - 0x003144f2 for the impact, 0x0031463e and 0x00314702 for
   the city's first two shots - and read 0x0033b0b2.

2.5.  The Rock

   Valley_StepScene eases three globals EXACTLY AS THE DEBRIS
   SCENE'S BODIES ARE EASED, which is the one piece of machinery
   the two scenes share [verified]:

       0x0033b070 -= it >> 3        x offset, staged 0x8c00
       0x0033b074 -= it >> 3        y offset, staged -0xc800
       0x0033b078 -= it >> 4        size,     staged 0xb400

       screen x = (x offset >> 8) + 0x50
       screen y = (y offset >> 8) + 0x41
       radius   = (size >> 8) + 0xa, clamped 0x7f

   so it enters off the top right at a clamped radius and eases in
   to (0x50, 0x41) at radius 10. A fuse at 0x0033b04a, staged
   0x270f, holds all of this still until the second loop sets it
   to 1 - which is why the opening is birds alone.

   THE STEPPER COMPUTES THE SCREEN TRIPLE AND THROWS IT AWAY. D1,
   D2 and D3 are built at 0x003164ce and the routine returns
   without using them; the second loop recomputes the same three
   from the globals at 0x00314454.

   VALLEY_DRAWROCK IS SPACE_DRAWLIGHTBLOB'S ALGORITHM, which is
   why the streak reads like the swirl [verified]:

       v = g_BlobFalloff[(radius << 8) | dist]
           + turb[cursor1 + dist]
           + turb[cursor2 + dist + (angle >> 2)]
       LIGHT += ramp[v]

   the falloff warped by two turbulence samples, one at the
   distance and one at the distance plus a QUARTER OF THE ANGLE.
   The swirl halves its height into an ellipse; this takes the
   span straight, so it is a circle. Section 2.7 still holds - it
   is a copy of that algorithm, not a call to it.

   THE RAMP INDEX IS A SIGNED WORD AND RUNS OFF BOTH ENDS. The
   turbulence is about +/-52 either side of a 0..255 falloff, so
   the index reaches roughly -52 to 307. The 0x50 bytes before
   0x0033b79e are 0x3f and the bytes after it are zero, so the
   core saturates and the rim fades out. Masking the index to
   0xff instead wraps the rim onto the ramp's bright head and
   draws a RING rather than a meteor [observed].

   0x00314102 builds the turbulence: 1024 words, each a
   Valley_Rand(0x1388) less 0x9c4 times four, plus two sine walks
   whose phases advance 0x80 and 0x100 an entry. Both cursors
   start at the table's head and the stepper walks one forward a
   word and the other back two, over the first 512.

   The trail is Valley_SpawnTrail and Valley_DrawTrail - 0x1f
   slots of 8 bytes at 0x0033b10e holding lifetime 0x4b, x, y and
   the rock's radius. THE SPAWN REFUSES WHILE THE RADIUS IS ABOVE
   0x7f, so nothing is laid until the rock is on screen, and the
   draw drops a slot when its SIZE reaches zero rather than its
   lifetime. Each puff is a blob of (size >> 2) plus
   Valley_Rand(2) through its own ramp at 0x0033b9c6, which
   0x0031409a builds as (0xff - i) >> 4 clamped - topping out at
   15, well under the head.

2.6.  Two Scenes, Two Generators

   Valley_Rand 0x0031586c is the Stills module's own: a linear
   congruential step on 0x0033b060, state * 0x5d43 + 0x5c2d, with
   the halves folded by eor before the range is taken. It is not
   the space module's Math_Rand 0x002cfe38 and shares no state
   with it [verified].

3.  The Reconstruction

   src/part_valley.c, registered as part 2.17. It needs no
   relic.dat: the picture is in the executable.

   ITS OWN FILE BECAUSE THE DEMO'S IS. Section 2.7 found the Stills
   module carrying its own copy of the blob skeleton, its own
   runtime tables and its own generator, so the port keeps the two
   apart rather than pointing this scene at the space module's
   primitives. Nothing in src/gfx.c is reused here.

   THERE IS NO IFF DECODER AND NONE IS NEEDED. Valley_LoadDeepImage
   scans the FORM A BYTE AT A TIME for 'DBOD' rather than walking
   chunk headers, and the body is three plain bytes a pixel with no
   compression - 16000 of them, which is the FORM's 0xbbc4 almost
   exactly. The three become b0.b1.00.b2 masked 0xfcfcfcfc and
   shifted down two, so the picture arrives as 6-bit colour with a
   zero LIGHT byte [verified].

   Scored against hardware at our tick 20, raw to raw:

           R 0.12   G 0.11   LIGHT 0.00   B 0.12

   with 15202 of 16000 pixels byte identical [observed]. Both
   light planes are zero, as section 2 requires of the opening.

   THE BIRDS' FIRST SINE TERM IS HALVED. 0x00314a2e takes the
   second phase's pair down one before the third phase's is added,
   so the displacement is (sin(a2) >> 1) + sin(a3), not their plain
   sum. Reading it as a sum moves every bird.

   THE ROCK AND ITS TRAIL ARE IN. Scored against hardware at
   our tick 429, where our rock's three counters are bit for bit
   the demo's - 749, -1062 and 7095, giving screen (82, 60) and
   radius 37:

           R 0.16   G 0.14   LIGHT 0.78   B 0.12

   with 11181 of 16000 byte identical and the LIGHT peak exact at
   117 [observed].

   THE SETUP STAGES THE BIRD DRIFT AT -0x64, not zero - 0x00313ff0
   - so the flock enters from the left rather than starting
   mid-screen.

   THE IMPACT IS IN - all five of its calls. Score it where
   0x0033b050 reads 1 and the threshold 0x2ba puts the frame 278
   fields into the impact's 290. At
   our tick 712,

           R 2.12   G 1.70   LIGHT 0.28   B 1.45

   [observed]. The haze's band matches in place and extent.

   A FRAME WITH 0x0033b050 == 3 IS THE CITY'S FIRST SHOT, NOT THE
   IMPACT, and that shot has no blast and no base pass in it at
   all. Scoring the impact against one measures two different
   scenes.

   THE SCENE ENDS WITH THE IMPACT. 0x00314572 leaves the third
   loop at 0x122 fields and falls straight into the city's
   preamble - there is no fourth loop, and the 0x0033b050 == 3
   that hardware shows is the city's first shot staging its own
   rate. Twelve fields further on the demo is out of the valley
   and seven more put it in the city, which lands the
   scene's end on our tick 725 [observed].

   How long the opening runs before the rock is released is OURS -
   the demo leaves its first loop on a cue we do not read.

2.8.  The Blast

   0x00315524 is the impact's fireball and it is UNLIKE EVERY OTHER
   BLOB IN THIS SCENE: the inner loop ends add.l D5,(A6)+, so it
   adds a whole longword and writes COLOUR, not just LIGHT
   [verified].

       v = g_BlobFalloff[(radius << 8) | dist]
           - rays[angle + spin]
           - rays[angle + 2 * spin]
       pixel += ramp[v]

   The warp is SUBTRACTIVE AND ANGULAR where the rock's is additive
   and radial, and that is what cuts spokes into the fireball. The
   spin is the low byte of 0x0033b048, which the stepper advances
   0xa a field, so the spokes turn.

   Its radius is 0x0033b07c >> 3 clamped to 0x7f, and 0x0033b07c
   counts down one a field from 0x3fc once the fuse is clear - so
   the fireball SHRINKS through the impact rather than growing.

   0x00314162 builds the rays: twenty runs of a Valley_Rand height
   written over a Valley_Rand span of angles, then each entry
   averaged with its neighbour - BY A lsr.b THAT TOUCHES THE LOW
   BYTE ONLY - AND NEGATED, by the neg.w at 0x003141ce. The table
   is therefore at or below zero, so the two subtractions above
   RAISE v and push the sample further down the ramp.

   Build it positive and the sign inverts the whole effect: v falls
   instead of rising, every texel out to the clip circle lands in
   the ramp's white head, and the fireball comes out a flat bright
   DISC rather than a glow with a falling edge [observed].

   0x003141da builds the colour ramp with the generator
   Gfx_BuildColourRamp already transcribes - 0x7f, 0x3f, 0x3f over
   0x780000, packed R.G.00.B. IT RUNS BRIGHT TO DARK: entry 0 is
   0x3f3f003f and entry 0xff is 0x05020002, so a LOW v is bright.

   THE INDEX IS NOT CLAMPED. 0x00315604 reads the ramp at a
   sign-extended word, and the memory past the 256th entry is zero,
   so a v that runs off the end adds nothing at all. Pinning it to
   the last entry instead paints a dim rim the demo does not
   have.

   THE TRAIL SLOTS AND THE RAMP OVERLAP. 0x0033b10e holds 0x1f
   slots of 8 bytes, which reaches 0x0033b206, and the ramp starts
   at 0x0033b1fe. Valley_DrawTrail walks 0x1e of them and stops
   short of it; Valley_SpawnTrail scans 0x1f and can write the
   ramp's first entry when all thirty are busy.

2.9.  The Impact's Base Pass

   Valley_ImpactBase 0x00315b5a replaces Valley_BlitBase in phase
   three rather than following it, and does two things in one walk
   of 0x62 by 0xa0 [verified].

   THE SPLAT is a BAKED byte map at 0x00322314 - a smooth bump
   peaking near the impact, 252 at its centre on row 65 falling to
   217 at the frame's edges. Where map[i] less the threshold is
   non-negative the picture is HALVED and a longword from the
   splat table added. Valley_BuildSplatTable 0x00315b22 builds
   that from the warm palette at 0x0033aa34 through the same
   b0.b1.00.b2 rotate the picture decode uses, so the scorched
   lens IS the palette, shaped by the map.

   THE PALETTE'S ROTATE IS NOT THE PICTURE'S. Its entries are
   00.RR.GG.BB longwords and the rotate turns them into
   RR.GG.00.BB; the picture's path reads three bytes and reaches
   the same result from a different source shape. Treating the
   palette the picture's way picks up 00.RR.00.GG and the lens
   comes out the wrong colour.

   THE THRESHOLD FALLS, AND THAT IS THE EXPANDING RING. 0x00316476
   takes 0x0033b050 off 0x0031654c every field and floors it at
   zero - one a field in the impact, THREE in the aftermath. As it
   drops, more of the map clears it and the lens spreads outward
   from the point of impact. Seen edge on that reads as a ring of
   fire opening across the valley. The impact stages 0x3d0 and the
   aftermath 0x320; hardware reads 0x2d5 [observed].

   THE WHITENING is the same pass. Every pixel takes or.w of
   0x0033b080, whose HIGH byte lands on LIGHT, so the whole frame
   gets a light floor of clamp(0x0033b07c >> 5, >= 0x12). It reads
   0x1d on hardware and falls as the blast shrinks. Without it
   the frame scores LIGHT 28.84 against 0.50 with it [observed].

2.10.  The Haze

   Valley_DrawHaze 0x00315e20 lays a perspective plasma on the
   ground, LIGHT ONLY. Both the impact and the aftermath call it;
   the impact passes the threshold DOUBLED and the aftermath
   passes it straight [verified].

   IT IS TWO PASSES, MIRRORED ABOUT THE HORIZON. The first walks
   UPWARD FROM ROW 49, each row advancing 0x280 and then
   subtracting 0x500 - one row up - with the height map walking
   alongside from +0x1ea0, and takes the row step off its
   accumulator. The second, at 0x00315f04, walks DOWNWARD FROM ROW
   50 for the same count, its map from +0x1f40, and ADDS the row
   step instead. Both carry the pan's row offset on the map
   pointer, so the gate tracks a scroll.

   Missing the second pass costs the whole lower half of the
   plasma. The row count is 0xaa0000 over the caller's term
   clamped under 0x32, so a nearer horizon draws fewer rows - and
   since that clamp bites for any term under about 2730 the two
   passes are 49 rows each in practice.

   THE OFFSET BETWEEN THE TWO SAMPLES CAN DRIFT, AND IN THE VALLEY
   IT DOES NOT. 0x00315f66 is seeded 0x15e at the scene's setup and
   0x00316494 adds 0x00315f68 to it once a field - but that step is
   CLEARED at 0x00314392 for the whole valley and only put to 1 at
   0x003145fa, on the way into the city. The valley's plasma is
   still and the city's crawls [verified].

   THE TEXTURE IS FPLASMA AT +0x20200 - the same chunk and the
   same bias the debris scene's Texture_GlowLight reads, through
   this module's own pointer at 0x0031670c. Two samples a texel,
   the first SWAPPED before the second is added, and only the low
   byte reaches LIGHT.

   AND THE TEXTURE IS NOT THE ARCHIVE'S. Valley_DarkenPlasma
   0x00315660 runs once from the scene's setup at 0x00314334 and
   rewrites the chunk IN PLACE. It reads the 0x10000 longwords as
   a 256 by 256 grid, takes max(0, |row| - 0x28) off every byte of
   each row with the row index running -0x80 to 0x7f, and floors
   each byte at zero. The plasma therefore keeps its middle 81
   rows and fades to nothing at both edges [verified; three probe
   points reproduce the live buffer exactly].

   THAT FADE IS WHAT ENDS THE LOWER PASS. The pass walks the row
   index out past 0x28 into the faded part, so its contribution
   dies away on its own instead of stopping at a row. Read the
   archive's copy instead and the haze ends on a HARD HORIZONTAL
   EDGE across the screen, at whatever row the count runs out
   [observed]. The debris scene reads the same chunk but runs
   first, so it sees it undarkened.

   IT IS A LATE EFFECT, and that is why it looks absent early.
   Each texel is gated on the height map clearing the threshold,
   and the map peaks at 190 in the rows the haze covers - row 30
   is zero and row 49 runs 172 to 190 - while the threshold starts
   at 244. Nothing draws until about 216 fields into the impact.

2.11.  Every Loop Ends On A FIELD Count

   0x00237218 is stepped by Vbl_Server 0x0021f9e4, so it counts
   FIELDS, not drawn frames [verified]. Both this scene's loops and
   the city's three shots end by comparing it - 0x122 here, 0xe6
   and 0x96 there - and the demo re-reads it inside the loop, so a
   frame that takes two fields to draw still costs two.

   A reconstruction that counts its own drawn frames instead runs
   every one of those loops too long on any host that cannot hold
   the field rate, and the overrun is invisible at 1:1.

2.7.  The Glows Are Copied, Not Shared

   The rock, the impact and the space module's blobs all look
   alike because THE SKELETON IS DUPLICATED PER SCENE, not
   parameterised and not called [verified]:

   -  Valley_DrawRock has NO callees. It carries its own copy of
      the clip prologue, the row walk and the 68020 memory
      indirect span read.
   -  It reads the SAME BAKED TABLES as the space module -
      g_PolarTable + 0x10000 and g_BlobFalloff - because those sit
      in the executable and are addressed absolutely.
   -  It reads a DIFFERENT RUNTIME TABLE. Its span half width
      comes through 0x0033b0d6 where the space module's comes
      through 0x002f1024, and on hardware those hold
      0x007a8fe8 and 0x005a2620 - two separate allocations of the
      same table [observed].

   So each scene owns its own code and its own runtime tables, and
   shares only what is baked. A reconstruction cannot assume one
   blob routine serves both: the prologues agree, the per-pixel
   tails do not.

4.  Open questions

   -  THE THIRD LOOP HAS NOT BEEN CAPTURED. What is on hand reads
      0x0033b050 == 3, which is the aftermath. A frame taken while
      that global is 1 - the white-out with the fireball still
      drawn - is what the impact needs before it can be scored.

   -  THE AFTERMATH'S 0x00315c34 AND 0x0031589e ARE UNREAD, as is
      Valley_DrawHaze 0x00315e20, which both of the last two loops
      call. The haze writes LIGHT only, adding a longword's low
      byte over rows 28 to 49 walked UPWARD from row 49, with its
      row count 0xaa0000 over a perspective term.

   -  WHAT HAS BEEN MEASURED. The opening, birds and lightning
      only. The rock 29 fields into its fall - its x offset and
      its size both give 29 against the staged 0x8c00 and 0xb400 -
      at screen (82, 60) and radius 37, with the size still above
      the 0x1400 that ends the phase. And one past the impact:
      0x0033b04e cleared, 0x0033b044 set and 0x0033b050 at 1
      [observed].

   -  VALLEY_DRAWHAZE 0x00315e20 IS NOT READ, and it is the whole
      of the impact's remaining colour error. It divides 0xaa0000
      by a perspective term, starts at frame offset 0x7a80 - row
      49, the horizon - and walks the same height map from the
      matching offset, so it is a ground plane rather than a blob.

   -  WHAT 0x003160c0 RESOLVES. Valley_PostPass waits on a
      semaphore, blurs, then hands *(0x0033b0b2) and *(0x0033b058)
      to it before signalling and bumping a frame counter. It is
      taken as the HAM8 resolve because the snapshot path at
      0x00315f96 - bit 2 of 0x00dff016, the right mouse button -
      passes it the same buffer, but it is not read.

   -  WHAT 0x00315660, 0x00315ace, 0x00315b22 AND 0x0031667a DO.
      All four are called once from the setup; the third is handed
      a palette at 0x0033aa34 whose first entries are black and
      warm oranges.

   -  WHETHER THE SCENE USES A SECOND BUFFER THE WAY THE DEBRIS
      SCENE DOES. 0x0033b0ae and 0x0033b0b2 are both read by the
      blur, and the triple-buffer table amiga_fb knows is the space
      module's, not this one's - reading a frame here needs the
      pointer at 0x0033b0ae, not a buffer index.
