PCDEMOS RELIC                                              Analysis Note


                       THE PLANET'S DESTRUCTION


Abstract

   The demo's second to last scene: the planet centred in frame with
   the flat vessel in front of it and the far side coming apart,
   then a white flash and a still of the aftermath.

   IT IS THE SPACE MODULE'S THIRD MODE, not a new one. Everything
   it draws except its glow is machinery the opening space scenes
   and the return flight already built, and the glow itself is a
   second copy of the valley's haze.

1.  Where It Sits

   Demo_RunAct2 0x0021f8a6 calls SpaceModule_Run with D0 = 2, between
   the stills module and the temple module's second call [verified]:

       +------------+-----------------------+------------------------+
       | 0x0021f860 | Sand_RunScene         |                        |
       | 0x0021f872 | TempleModule_Run  0   | the ruins              |
       | 0x0021f884 | SpaceModule_Run   1   | the return flight      |
       | 0x0021f894 | Stills_RunModule      | the valley and city    |
       | 0x0021f8a6 | SpaceModule_Run   2   | THIS SCENE             |
       | 0x0021f8b8 | TempleModule_Run  1   | the ark's departure    |
       +------------+-----------------------+------------------------+

   Space_RunDestruction 0x002c8438 is the mode's body.

   THE PLANET IS AT THE WORLD ORIGIN and the camera's yaw and pitch
   are both cleared, which is why it sits centred here where every
   earlier space scene has it at an edge [verified]. g_SceneMode is
   6; the return flight's is 1.

2.  The Two Phases

   Space_RunDestruction runs two loops with a white flash between
   them [verified]:

       1  Destruct_DrawFrame 0x002c887c, while 0x002c8c98 is above
          -0x385. That counter is staged at 200 and falls ten a
          field, so the phase lasts 110 fields.

       2  Space_FillWhite, presented twice.

       3  Destruct_DrawAftermath 0x002c88c8, with the counter reset
          to zero and the scene fuse to 9999, until the music cue.

   THE SCENE OPENS ON A BLANK SCREEN IT DID NOT INSTALL, and its
   counters do not start with it. The wait on (0x26,0x34) at
   0x002c844e returns seven fields before the copper list goes in at
   0x002d542e, the city's exit left a list on the chip that fetches
   no bitplane, and so those seven fields are black - effects/
   city.txt 5.2 [observed].

   PHASE ONE IS FOUR CALLS over a cleared frame:

       Draw_Flare
       Scene_DrawPlanetAndHalo
       Space_BlitPortalSprite      the flat vessel
       Destruct_DrawGlow

   AND A FIFTH PASS NO SCENE'S CALL LIST MENTIONS. Space_PresentFrame
   0x002d5520 runs Space_BlurFrame 0x002ce992 before Ham8_EncodeFrame,
   so EVERY frame of EVERY space scene is blurred - five spatial taps
   and the destination's previous contents, each folded in by halves,
   the same routine Valley_BlurFrame is for the stills module.

   IT IS EASY TO MISS ENTIRELY, because it lives in the present rather
   than in the scene. Adding it to this scene moved the planet frame's
   LIGHT from 1.46 to 0.41 and took something off every channel of all
   three landings [observed].

   THE BLUR WRITES THE BUFFER THAT IS PRESENTED. It reads
   g_pFrameBuffer 0x002f101c and writes g_pFrameBufferBack 0x002f1020,
   so a reference capture taken from the first of those is PRE-BLUR
   and will not match a faithful render - the same trap the stills
   module has at 0x0033b0ae, see effects/valley.txt 2.4.

   PHASE TWO IS A STILL. It blits 160 by 99 longwords from
   g_pRingTexture + 0x116e8 and forces byte +2 to 0x002c8622 >> 2 as
   it goes, so the picture arrives with a UNIFORM LIGHT level rather
   than one of its own. Then the flare and Destruct_DrawParticles.

   THE SOURCE IS 256 LONGWORDS WIDE, NOT 160. The row advance at
   0x002c88bc is +0x61 after 160 columns, so the still is a window
   into a 256 wide grid; reading it at 160 bands the picture into
   horizontal stripes [verified].

   0x002c8628 IS THE FLARE'S RADIUS AND IT NEVER FADES. It is staged
   at 0x7f and the subtraction at 0x002c8916 sits behind a bge that
   skips it whenever the value is positive - which it always is - so
   the flare holds at 0x7f for the whole phase.

3.  The Glow Is The Valley's Haze Again

   Destruct_DrawHaze 0x002c8ca2 IS Valley_DrawHaze 0x00315e20,
   duplicated rather than shared - which is what the modules do with
   every other glow in this corpus, see effects/valley.txt 2.7.

   Every constant is the same [verified]: the term clamped at 0x14,
   0xaa0000 over it for the span, the row count clamped under 0x32,
   -0xc0000/d4 for the column start, 0x320000/d4 rotated eight for
   the column step, 0x640000/d4 for the row step, the frame entered
   at +0x7a80 which is row 49, and the two passes mirrored about the
   horizon - the first walking up and taking the row step off its
   accumulator, the second entered at +32000, row 50, walking down
   and adding it. Two samples a texel from +0x20200 of the texture,
   the second displaced by a drifting offset.

   THREE THINGS DIFFER:

       +-------------+--------------------+----------------------+
       | what        | the valley         | here                 |
       +=============+====================+======================+
       | texture     | FPLASMA            | g_pRingTexture       |
       | gate map    | the height map     | 0x002eb130 up,       |
       |             |                    | 0x002eb1d0 down      |
       | the write   | add.b, WRAPS       | g_LightSatLut,       |
       |             |                    | SATURATES            |
       +-------------+--------------------+----------------------+

   The drifting offset is g_RingPhaseA where the valley's is
   g_HazeSampleOffset.

   Destruct_DrawGlow 0x002c8c52 latches the gate from 0x002c8c9e into
   0x002c8c9a and calls the haze THREE TIMES a frame, at three
   depths - the timer plus 0x352, the timer plus 0x4e2, and a flat
   0xc8 - which is what layers it.

   THE TEXTURE IS FPLASMA, the same chunk the valley's haze reads,
   and Destruct_DarkenPlasma 0x002ccb08 vignettes it exactly as
   Valley_DarkenPlasma does with ONE constant changed: the keep-band
   is 0x1e rows here against the valley's 0x28, so this scene's
   plasma fades sooner at the edges [verified].

   THE GATE MAP IS BAKED, not built - a radial field at
   g_DestructGateField 0x002e9290 with a HOLE WHERE THE PLANET SITS,
   which is why the glow never draws over the disc. The two pass
   pointers 0x002eb130 and 0x002eb1d0 are +0x1ea0 and +0x1f40 into
   it, the same offsets the valley uses into its height map, and the
   row at +0x1ea0 falls away from 0xff starting at column 48 -
   exactly where the planet's disc begins [observed].

   The gate falls from 0xff, and the test is gate <= map, so the
   glow starts over the field's 0xff plateau and spreads into the
   falloff as the gate drops.

4.  The Debris Is The Aftermath

   Destruct_SeedParticles 0x002c867e fills 3000 nodes of 0x20 bytes
   at (0x002f10f8), once, in the scene's setup. THE VELOCITY IS
   RADIAL [verified]:

       a     = Math_Rand(0x2000)          13 bits, the table's range
       speed = Math_Rand(0xd2) + 4
       vx    = (sin[a] * speed) >> 13     0x228b6c
       vy    = (cos[a] * speed) >> 13     0x228b6c + 0x2000

   so the debris leaves evenly in every direction and the spread of
   speeds is what stretches it into streaks. The rest of the node is
   a z of Math_Rand(0x7d0) + 0xfa0, a radius of Math_Rand(0x1e) +
   0x14, an index, a colour that is zero for four nodes in five, and
   a KIND drawn from the 100-entry weighting table at 0x002c873c.

   Destruct_DrawParticles 0x002c8974 projects them, and it is called
   ONLY FROM THE AFTERMATH - the first phase never draws one.

       z  = node.z - 0x002c8624          the advance, +10 a field
       skip while z < 0x65
       sx = ((vx << 8) / z) + 0x50
       sy = ((vy << 8) / z) + 0x32

   THE MOTION IS ENTIRELY IN Z. vx and vy never change; the debris
   appears to fly outward because the perspective divide shrinks as
   the advance eats the z.

   LIGHT RIDES THE SAME Z, through the idiom this scene uses twice:
   0x002c89cc takes 3000 off it and ZEROES THE TERM WHILE IT IS
   POSITIVE, so LIGHT holds at 0x3f until z falls under 3000 and
   then fades as the debris nears. The result is shifted up a byte
   and OR'd into every pixel written.

   The kinds, and TWO OF THEM NEVER DRAW:

       +------+-------+--------------------------------------------+
       | kind | of 80 | what is drawn                              |
       +======+=======+============================================+
       |  0   |   55  | one pixel in the node's own colour         |
       |  1   |    2  | Draw_FlareTurbulent on g_RampBeamHead,     |
       |      |       | gated on the radius projecting under 0x7f  |
       |  2   |    9  | a 6 x 5 sprite, frame                      |
       |      |       | (index + 0x002e0c7c / 2) & 3               |
       |  3   |    9  | the same, from the second bank of four     |
       |  4   |    5  | NOTHING - see below                        |
       +------+-------+--------------------------------------------+

   THE KIND TABLE HAS EIGHTY ENTRIES AND IS INDEXED OVER A HUNDRED.
   Destruct_DrawFrame begins at 0x002c887c, eighty longwords past
   0x002c873c, so Math_Rand(0x64) sends one node in five to read its
   kind out of that routine's own code. Those values match nothing
   in the dispatch and the node never draws [verified].

   AND KIND 4 IS UNREACHABLE. The test at 0x002c8a0a repeats #3,
   which the branch above it has already taken, so 0x002c8b86 is
   dead and the five entries giving kind 4 draw nothing either.
   Ghidra renders that as a duplicated kind-3 branch rather than as
   unreachable code.

   Between them, 25 of every 100 nodes are invisible.

   0x2c8a18 IS THE LOOP'S CONTINUE LABEL, not a ramp - the routine
   pushes it with pea and each kind rts's back to it.

   Destruct_BuildSpriteSheet 0x002c8630 builds the sheet at
   (0x002f116c) from a 768-byte index table through a palette,
   dividing every byte by 8 and then adding 0x08040002 back to the
   first 193 entries. Its row stride is 0x20 longwords and zero
   texels are skipped, so the sprites carry their own mask.

6.  The Reconstruction

   src/part_destruction.c, part 2.19. The first phase only.

   ITS COUNTERS MATCH. Hardware holds the timer at -180 and the
   glow's gate still shut at 0xff, which is our tick 39 exactly
   [observed], and the planet, its mask and the flat vessel land on
   the same pixels - the frame's row 50 agrees across the whole disc,
   x 54 to 108.

   THE GATE IS ZEROED WHILE ITS TERM IS POSITIVE, not while it is
   negative. 0x002d5946 adds 0x280 to the timer AFTER the field's
   subtraction and clears the term on ble, so the gate sits at 0xff
   and the glow draws nothing at all until the timer passes -0x280,
   about 64 fields in. A decompiler reading of that block inverts it
   and opens the glow immediately.

   TWO CONSTANTS THE SETUP RESTAGES AND A PORT WILL ASSUME WRONG.
   0x002c8558 passes Ramp_BuildColour a numerator of 0xfa0000 for
   the amber ramp, nearly double the 0x780000 Ramp_BuildAll gives
   it, and that is what carries the flare out to the frame's edges;
   and 0x002c8502 hands Ham8_BuildTables 0x6f / 0x4f / 0x3f, which
   is the opening scenes' base REVERSED, so light here washes toward
   a warm white rather than a blue one [verified].

   Scored against hardware at tick 39:

           R 3.63   G 3.76   LIGHT 1.46   B 2.78

   [observed]. The glow's hole for the planet lands within a pixel
   of the demo's on both edges - 49 to 110 against our 48 to 111 at
   row 50 - and the planet, its mask and the flat vessel are on the
   same pixels across the whole disc.

   THE AFTERMATH IS IN TOO. Two readings sit 22 and 318 fields
   into it, and the advance at 0x002c8624 reads 1340 and 4300 at
   those points against our 1340 and 4300 [observed]:

       advance 1340    R 8.88   G 8.77   LIGHT 4.79   B 8.02
       advance 4300    R 13.75  G 12.61  LIGHT 12.76  B 11.48

   The debris cloud's size, place and character match; its
   PLACEMENT cannot, since every node's angle, speed and depth come
   from the generator. The later frame scores worse for that reason
   alone - by then all three thousand are spread over the frame.

   THE FLARE'S RAY PHASE CANNOT BE MATCHED and need not be. It is a
   running counter the scene only increments - hardware reads
   0x4cec - but g_RayArcs is filled by the generator, so the arcs
   sit in different places whatever the phase. Staging the observed
   value makes the score slightly worse, so the reconstruction
   leaves it at zero.

7.  Open questions

   -  THE OTHER SPACE PARTS DO NOT APPLY THE BLUR. Every scene that
      calls Space_PresentFrame gets it, which is all of them, but
      only this part's reconstruction runs it. Adding it to
      src/part_return.c scores WORSE against that part's references
      - the burst's LIGHT goes from 0.06 to 2.54 - which says those
      references were captured from g_pFrameBuffer before the blur,
      not that the blur is wrong there. They need re-capturing from
      g_pFrameBufferBack before the question can be settled, which
      means capturing the scene again.

   -  HOW LONG THE AFTERMATH RUNS. Its loop ends on a music cue, as
      the city's third shot does, so the length is ours; we run it
      until the advance has passed every node's z.
