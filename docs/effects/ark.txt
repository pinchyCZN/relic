PCDEMOS RELIC                                              Analysis Note


                        THE ARK'S DEPARTURE


Abstract

   The demo's last scene: the ark climbing away over the warped
   ground into a light, and the screen washing to white around it.

   IT IS THE TEMPLE MODULE'S SECOND MODE, not a new one, and the
   temple scene's machinery draws nearly all of it. Nothing in it
   waits on the music - the whole scene is three counters.

1.  Where It Sits

   Demo_RunAct2 0x0021f8b8 calls TempleModule_Run with D0 = 1, after
   the planet's destruction and last of all [verified]. The module
   dispatches at 0x003678e0: D0 == 0 is the ruins, D0 == 1 falls to
   0x0036822e, which is Temple_InitMode1 and then a loop on
   Temple_StepMode1 until the quit flag.

   THE SCENE IS 0x47e FIELDS LONG. 0x00368284 stages g_ArkTicksLeft
   at 0x47e and Temple_StepMode1Camera takes one off it a field,
   raising the quit flag at zero. There is no cue to read and no
   choice for a reconstruction to make - unlike the city's third
   shot or the destruction's aftermath.

2.  The Frame

   Temple_StepMode1 0x003682f8 latches the camera and object state
   the VBL hook advanced, then makes seven calls [verified]:

       +------------+------------------------+-------------------+
       | address    | routine                | what it does      |
       +============+========================+===================+
       | 0x00367d3a | Temple_WarpTerrain     | the whole frame   |
       | 0x00369f7e | Temple_DrawSkyHaze     | LIGHT, REPLACED   |
       | 0x0036cf02 | Temple_DrawDustPuff    | a blob, r = 0x46  |
       | 0x0036b59e | Temple_DrawLightBlob   | a blob, r = 0x7f  |
       | 0x0036843a | Temple_DrawObjectRange | objects 0 .. 0xc  |
       | 0x003683d8 | Temple_Mode1Fade       | the frame's LIGHT |
       +------------+------------------------+-------------------+

   and Temple_RunMode1's loop follows each one with Temple_BlurFrame
   0x0036e238, which is the module's present pass - section 6.

   THE ORDER MATTERS TWICE OVER. The terrain writes every pixel as a
   whole longword, so nothing clears the frame and nothing may be
   drawn before it; and the sky haze REPLACES the light byte, so it
   is the floor both blobs and the fade then build on.

   TEN OBJECTS, NOT THIRTEEN. The range is 0 to 0xc, but every record
   whose +0x40 is non-zero is skipped, and Temple_InitMode1 pours
   g_TempleObjectMask 0x0036a152 into that field at 0x0036a0fe: it
   hides objects 4, 5 and 10 [verified]. Drawing all thirteen puts a
   mast on the ark that the demo has not got.

   THE LIGHT BLOB IS NAILED TO THE SHIP. Its centre is the FIRST
   PROJECTED POINT of object 9, less 0x12 across and plus 8 down -
   Temple_StepMode1 reads it out of that object's own point buffer at
   0x003683a4, which the previous frame's Temple_DrawObjectRange
   filled, so the glow is one field behind the hull it sits on
   [verified]. On hardware the buffer reads 0x92, 0x3b and the
   reconstruction projects 145, 59 for the same vertex [observed].

   WATCH THE RADIUS AT 0x00368356. A perspective radius is divided
   out of 0x186a0 by 0x00372b1a and then THROWN AWAY two
   instructions later by a flat move.l #0x46,D1, which also makes
   the clamp under it dead. A decompiler shows the divide as live
   code and the dust puff as perspective-scaled; it is not.

3.  One Counter Does Both Fades

   g_ArkFadeLevel 0x003726d6 is the scene's whole light curve, and
   it runs in two directions [verified]:

       staged 0x200 by 0x00368284
       falls 4 a field while the camera's Z is above -64999
       CLIMBS 1 a field once Z passes under it
       floors at zero

   So it opens the scene by fading IN from white over its first 128
   fields, sits at zero through the middle, and then WHITES OUT at
   the end. Temple_Mode1Fade 0x003683d8 turns it into the picture:
   every one of 0x3de0 longwords has min(0x3f, level >> 3) added to
   its light byte, through g_TempleSatRamp so it saturates rather
   than wrapping, and the pass is skipped entirely at zero.

   THE CAMERA IS WHAT TRIPS IT. 0x00372b88 is staged at 0xfffff060,
   which is -4000 DECIMAL, and Temple_StepMode1Camera takes 0x8c off
   it a field - so it reaches -64999 at field 436, which is where the
   white out begins.

   Three readings on hardware confirm the whole model [observed]:

       +------+--------+-------+--------+------------------------+
       | slot | fields | fade  | cam Z  | predicted              |
       +======+========+=======+========+========================+
       |  4   |     24 |   416 |      - | 0x200 - 4 * 24 = 416   |
       |  6   |    821 |   386 |-118940 | 821 - 436 = 385, and   |
       |      |        |       |        | -4000 - 0x8c * 821     |
       +------+--------+-------+--------+------------------------+

   The camera matches to the byte and the fade to within one field.

4.  The Two Blobs

   BOTH ARE THE SAME SKELETON every scene in this corpus duplicates
   - Temple_DrawLightBlob 0x0036b59e is Valley_DrawBlast
   0x00315524's arithmetic exactly [verified]:

       v = g_BlobFalloff[(radius << 8) | dist]
           - rays[angle + g_TempleBlobPhase]
           - rays[angle + 2 * g_TempleBlobPhase]
       pixel += ramp[v]

   with the angle and distance coming from g_AtanTable as a packed
   pair, the two angular samples SUBTRACTED, and the ramp added as a
   whole longword. Register arguments: D1 the radius, D2/D3 the
   centre, A1 the ray table, A4 the ramp.

   THEY DIFFER IN WHERE THEY SIT. The dust puff is nailed to the
   frame's centre, 0x50 by 0x32, at a fixed 0x46. The light blob
   follows object 9 - section 2.

4.1.  The Ray Table Is Exact

   Temple_SeedNoiseTables 0x00368118 fills both of them once per
   module, off Temple_Rand 0x003680e8. THE SEED IS CLEARED TWO
   INSTRUCTIONS EARLIER at 0x003679be and nothing else in the demo
   draws from that LCG, so the tables are the same in every run and a
   reconstruction reproduces them byte for byte rather than in
   character [verified against the live buffer].

       x    = x * 0x5d43 + 0x5c2d
       rand = (n * ((x >> 16) ^ x & 0xffff)) >> 16

   (0x0037471c) comes first - twenty five-wide spikes, read only by
   the type-4 object glow, which this scene's range never reaches.
   ITS FORTY DRAWS STILL MATTER, because they set where the second
   table's sequence begins.

   (0x00374720) is the light blob's: twenty arcs of a constant height
   0..0xc over a run of 0x15..0x24 angles, wrapping; then each entry
   averaged with the next - by an lsr.b, so the LOW BYTE only - and
   NEGATED at 0x003681c6. Valley_BuildRays is the same generator and
   negates for the same reason, effects/valley.txt 2.8.

   The third table at (0x00374724), a 2.4.8.4.2 kernel, is written
   and read by nothing.

5.  The Sky Is Two Scrolling Layers

   Temple_DrawSkyHaze 0x00369f7e writes the light byte of all 16000
   texels from a pair of longwords per texel out of (0x00374704):

       LIGHT = 0x0037c900[(angleB + b) as a word]
             + low byte of (0x003748f8)[+0x20000 + (angleA + a) * 4]

   The two angles are 0x00369f76 and 0x00369f7a, advanced +0x201 and
   -0x100 a field, so the layers crawl in opposite directions at
   different rates.

   THE PAIRS ARE A CENTRED POLAR FIELD. Temple_BuildHazeLayerA
   0x00369fd0 and Temple_BuildHazeLayerB 0x0036a038 fill them off
   g_AtanTable, indexed by a SIGNED word whose high byte is the row
   from -0x32 to 0x31 and whose low byte is the column from -0x50 to
   0x4f [verified]:

       a = angle byte, d = distance byte
       A: entry = a      | ((0x10000  / d) >> 8 & 0xff) << 8
       B: entry = a * 2  | ((0x320000 / d) >> 8 & 0xff) << 8

   The high byte is a RECIPROCAL of the distance, so each layer flows
   outward from the centre rather than sweeping across; B's doubled
   angle and 0x32-times numerator are the whole of the difference
   between them.

   THE TEXTURE IS FPLASMA AGAIN - the same chunk the valley's haze
   and the space module's glow read. Temple_InitMode1 loads it at
   0x0036a0a2 off the name list at 0x003e437c, and
   Temple_GreyHazeTexture 0x0036b880 then greyscales it in place:
   every longword's B byte doubled plus eight, splatted over the
   other channels with LIGHT left at zero. Fplasma's 06.04.00.16
   becomes 34.34.00.34, which is what the live buffer reads
   [verified].

   IT IS THE SCENE'S ONLY TEXTURE and both readers want the same
   bytes: the sky haze takes the one byte as a height, and the ground
   under it takes the whole longword as a pixel.

   0x0037c900 IS REAL FILE DATA, a wave profile, and it is indexed by
   a SIGNED word - so the negative half reads out of the table into
   whatever precedes it, the same way the destruction's kind table
   runs into code.

5.1.  The Ground Is The Backdrop

   Temple_WarpTerrain 0x00367d3a is not a strip along the bottom of
   the picture - it writes all 16000 pixels as whole longwords
   straight over the frame, and everything else in the scene is drawn
   on top of it. Leaving it out costs far more than the ark does: the
   demo's red channel averages 38.7 down the left of the frame where
   a reconstruction without it reads 3.8 [observed].

   It is a rotozoom over a 256 by 256 image. Three corners go through
   0x00367ce4 at g_TempleWarpAngle 0x00372b12 and g_TempleAltitude
   0x00372b1a, 0x12c0 for this scene; the differences become a column
   step over 0xa0 and a row step over 0x64. The texel index is MERGED
   out of two accumulators - bits 8..15 of the row one and bits 0..7
   of the ror'd column one - which is what wraps it, and the texture
   pointer is biased 0x20000 so the image's centre is the origin.

5.2.  Two Counters Are Handed Over

   Temple_RunMode1 0x0036822e stages a dozen globals and two of the
   ones the frame depends on are NOT among them. g_TempleBlobSpin
   0x00372bbc, which turns the light blob's rays, and
   g_TempleWarpAngle 0x00372b16, which turns the ground, are stepped
   by the ruins' VBL hook Temple_StepSceneCamera 0x00372524 as well
   as by this scene's, so both arrive carrying the whole module's
   history [verified].

   Hardware 151 fields in reads 0x758 and -2155. Winding those
   back through the +8 and +0xa the hook applies puts the scene's
   first field at 680 and -3655 [observed].

   THE TWO SKY-HAZE ANGLES BESIDE THEM ARE AT ZERO, because only mode
   1's hook touches 0x00369f76 and 0x00369f7a - which is the check
   that this is a real handover and not a misread tick count.

6.  The Reconstruction

   src/part_ark.c, part 2.20. THE COUNTERS ARE EXACT: at two points
   on hardware the demo reads ticks 1126 and 329, fade 416 and 386,
   and a
   camera Z of -118940, and the part reads the same six numbers at
   its ticks 25 and 822 [observed]. The white out opens on its tick
   437, a field after the predicted 436.

   BOTH RAMPS ARE EXACT TOO. Temple_BuildColourRampPlain 0x00367f1e
   is Ramp_BuildColour's curve instruction for instruction, so
   Gfx_BuildColourRamp covers it on 0x3f / 0x2f / 0x20 over 0xc80000;
   Temple_BuildPuffRamp 0x0036cffe is the same curve with a scale on
   the index and one byte out, on 0x1000000 over 0x12c.

   EVERY DRAW CALL IS IN. The terrain, the blur and the object range
   are part_temple.c's already and are reached through three entries
   added to src/objset.h - Temple_WarpTerrainTo, Temple_BlurFrameTo
   and Temple_DrawObjectRange - each the module's own routine over
   the host's frame rather than its fixed 160 by 100 one.

   Against the two readings, per channel [observed]:

       +-------+--------+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+
       | slot  | fields |     R |     G | LIGHT |     B |  same |
       +=======+========+=======+=======+=======+=======+=======+
       | 4     |     25 |  0.90 |  0.76 |  0.59 |  0.78 | 63.4% |
       | 5     |    151 |  2.01 |  1.70 |  1.37 |  1.44 |  9.8% |
       | 6     |    822 |  1.25 |  1.16 |  0.36 |  1.05 | 24.3% |
       +-------+--------+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+

   and the three counters read the same at all three: ticks left,
   fade level and camera Z agree to the unit.

   SCORE THE LAST FRAME OF A RUN, NEVER A SINGLE ONE. Half of
   Temple_BlurFrame is the frame it presented last, so one frame on
   its own comes out at half the demo's brightness however correct
   the rest of it is.

7.  Open questions

   -  WHETHER THE TWO HANDED-OVER COUNTERS CAN BE DERIVED rather
      than measured. src/part_ark.c carries section 5.2's figures as
      the scene's entry state; playing the module through from the
      ruins would produce them instead, if part_temple.c's ruins runs
      to the same length as the demo's.

   -  0x003720ec and 0x0037210a in the run are unread, as is
      0x0036d636 in Temple_InitMode1.

   -  Gfx_Flare PINS THE RAMP INDEX where Temple_DrawLightBlob reads
      a sign-extended word past the table into zeros, so the demo's
      core goes hollow where two rays push the falloff over 0xff and
      the reconstruction's saturates. Neither is visible at either
      reading; see effects/planet_sun.txt 6 for the other side of
      the same choice.
