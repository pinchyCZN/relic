PCDEMOS RELIC                                              Analysis Note


                      THE ORB FALLS TOWARD EARTH


Abstract

   The short shot that closes the space sequence: the camera holds on
   the planet and the sphere drops away toward it, shrinking.
   Space_RunEarth at 0x002cc0ba, anim mode 0xe.

   TWO THINGS HERE ARE NEW AND THE REST IS REUSE. The planet is a
   texture BUILT AT RUN TIME, not a chunk, and its blue limb is baked
   into that texture rather than drawn over the frame. Everything
   else - the orb, its cracks, the fullscreen fill - is machinery the
   release scene already uses, run with different parameters.

1.  The Scene

   SpaceModule_Part0 calls it straight after Space_RunRelease
   [verified, 0x002c8426]. One mode, 0xe, and two loops:

    +----+------+-------------------------+---------------------+
    | ph | ends on                        | render              |
    +====+================================+=====================+
    |  1 | a music cue, 0x0021ff64        | Scene_RenderEarth   |
    |  2 | g_Ham8ScrollY reaching 0x64    | Scene_RenderEarth   |
    +----+--------------------------------+---------------------+

   Phase 2 seeds g_Ham8ScrollY to 2 and runs until it reaches 100, so
   the shot ends on a vertical scroll rather than a fuse or a cue.

   THE SETUP IS WHAT MAKES IT A DIFFERENT SCENE [verified,
   0x002cc0dc onward]:

       g_OrbDepth         0x96     the orb starts near and recedes
       g_CrackThreshold   0x7d0    the cracks CLOSE - see section 3
       g_CrackPhaseA / B  0        both reset
       g_CameraYaw/Pitch  0        a fresh orientation
       g_WarpParamA       0

2.  The Planet Is Generated

   EARTH_BUILDTEXTURE MAKES IT IN THREE PASSES [verified,
   0x002d3026] and nothing in relic.dat holds the result.

   PASS ONE, 65536 texels, is a spherical remap of the SKY:

       d = g_pSkyDirTable[0x20200 + i * 4] + 0x118, masked 0x3ffff
       texel = g_pSkyTexture[d]

   so the body of the planet is the skybox re-projected through the
   same direction table Scene_DrawTexturedSky walks. That is why it
   does not look like the lit planet of the earlier scenes - it is
   not the same artwork.

   PASS TWO stamps g_pEarthOverlay at +0x8c00, 0x5c rows of 0xf0
   longwords on a 0x400 stride, SKIPPING ZEROS so the stamp carries
   transparency. 92 x 240 four-byte pixels is 0x15900 bytes, which is
   Fearth's size exactly - see formats/relic_dat.txt.

   PASS THREE IS THE ATMOSPHERE, and it is baked into the texture:

       r = Math_Isqrt(x*x + y*y)
       v = abs((r - 0x41a) * 2), capped at 0xff
       c = g_HaloRamp[v], each channel halved
       texel += c

   Because it is |r - 0x41a| the value peaks ON a circle and falls
   away to both sides, so it is a LIMB GLOW and not a disc. It adds
   rather than replaces, so it tints whatever the first two passes
   left underneath.

3.  What Is Reused, And How It Differs

   THE BACKDROP IS THE SAME FULLSCREEN AFFINE FILL as the release's
   hull shot. Warp_FullscreenRotate (0x002d3e94) is
   Warp_FullscreenAffine instruction for instruction except that its
   corners go through Gfx_Rotate2D instead of
   Warp_ScaleRotateCorner - rotation without the scale. Both read
   g_WarpParamA and g_WarpParamB; this one is handed
   g_pEarthTexture. effects/orb_release.txt 6 has the fill's own
   arithmetic, including the 24.8 index.

   THE ORB IS STILL FCRUST. Earth_LoadCrustTexture reloads it and
   points g_pOrbTexture at a second buffer [verified, 0x002cc1ea], so
   the falling sphere is the same cracked ball.

   AND THE CRACKS CLOSE. Crack_DrawShells culls a point unless BOTH
   its sine lookups clear g_CrackThreshold, and this scene seeds that
   0x7d0 where the release holds it at zero. Only points near the
   sine peak survive, so the network thins to a few glowing seams as
   the orb recedes.

   WHICH ALSO WAKES Crack_DrawMidpointGlow. That routine does nothing
   below a threshold of 0x64 [verified, 0x002cde4e] and so is inert
   through the whole release; here it fires, dropping a glow blob on
   each surviving edge's midpoint at half the orb's radius.

   THE ORB'S OWN GATES ARE THE RELEASE'S. Scene_RenderEarth projects
   it through Orb_ProjectAndFlare only while g_OrbDepth is at least
   0x96, and draws the cracks only while the projected radius is
   under 0x50 - the same two tests.

4.  The Rates

   MODE 0xe, one handler [verified, 0x002d5c66 Anim_SceneTick +0x578]:

    +---------------------------+--------------------------+
    | per tick                  | 0xe                      |
    +===========================+==========================+
    | g_WarpParamA              | -3                       |
    | g_CameraYaw               | +0x28                    |
    | g_CameraPitch             | +0xf                     |
    | g_FlatScroll              | -0x200                   |
    | g_CrackPhaseA             | +0x25                    |
    | g_CrackPhaseB             | +0x53                    |
    | g_CrackThreshold          | +0x1e                    |
    | g_OrbDepth                | +0xa                     |
    | 0x002e0c44                | +1                       |
    | 0x002e0c48                | -(sin[0x002e0c44] >> 3)  |
    +---------------------------+--------------------------+

   THE ORB RECEDES BECAUSE THE DEPTH RISES - this is the only scene
   where g_OrbDepth grows. The release drives it down by 2 a tick to
   bring the orb at the camera; here it climbs 0xa and the sphere
   shrinks away.

   AND THE CRACKS CLOSE AS IT GOES. g_CrackThreshold starts 0x7d0 and
   gains 0x1e a tick, so fewer points clear it every frame and the
   seams wink out one by one as the orb falls.

   VERIFIED AT TWO POINTS [observed, on hardware]:

       +------+----------------+--------------------+
       | tick | g_OrbDepth     | g_CrackThreshold   |
       +======+================+====================+
       |   31 | 460   = seed+310 | 2930 = seed+930  |
       |  240 | 2550  = seed+2400| 9200 = seed+7200 |
       +------+----------------+--------------------+

   Both are exact against the seeds at both points, 209 ticks apart.

   0x002e0c48 IS DRIVEN BY A SINE whose phase, 0x002e0c44, advances
   one a tick: the handler subtracts g_SinTable[phase] >> 3 from it
   each frame. Scene_RenderEarth reads it as the orb's x, shifted
   right eight, so the sphere drifts sideways on a slow curve rather
   than dropping straight.

   PHASE 2 CLOSES THE DISPLAY WINDOW, not the picture - THAT IS THE
   TRANSITION OUT OF THIS SCENE. While g_Ham8ScrollY is non-zero the
   handler patches two words into 0x002d5380 and 0x002d5382:

       DIWSTRT = ((0x3e + scrollY) << 8) | 0x83
       DIWSTOP = ((0x02 - scrollY) << 8) | 0xbf

   and adds 2 to the counter. So the top edge walks down and the
   bottom edge walks up together, and the shot ends by squeezing to a
   slit over the 49 ticks the counter needs to reach 0x64. The loop
   is still rendering the scene behind it, which is why nothing white
   is ever drawn: the picture is not covered, it is CROPPED.

   THE WINDOW IS 196 SCANLINES OVER THE FRAME'S 100 ROWS, so each
   edge takes one row a tick and the two meet exactly as the counter
   lands on 0x64. A port with no display window paints the same bars
   into the frame instead - that substitution is ours.

   PHASE 1 ENDS ON A MUSIC CUE and so has no length of its own.
   Space_RunEarth arms (0x29, 0x00) at 0x002cc13c and polls at
   0x002cc158 until the replayer passes it [verified].

   IT IS 323 FIELDS IN A CLEAN RUN, measured from the demo's first
   frame: Scene_RenderEarth's first call and Music_PollCue's consume
   are 865 and 1188 fields past the same point, and g_OrbDepth
   reads 0x0d34 at the cue, which is 0x96 plus ten a field for 323
   of them - two independent readings of the same number [observed].

   IT IS NOT A CONSTANT OF THE SCENE. A different playthrough gave
   601 for the same boundary, because how long
   phase 1 lasts depends on where the music had got to when the
   scene started. EA_PH2_AT carries 323 as the fallback a port with
   no replayer needs; the cue is the mechanism.

2.1.  A Light Sphere Lies Over The Whole Planet

   WITHOUT IT THE TEXTURE'S DARK TEXELS READ AS HOLES.
   Warp_FullscreenAffine copies WHOLE LONGWORDS out of the planet
   texture, and the texture's light byte is zero in every texel - so
   the warp alone leaves the frame with no light at all, and any
   texel the planet's night side contributes comes out near black.

   Scene_RenderEarth draws Space_DrawSunBlob 0x002d20fa over it
   [verified, 0x002cdef0]: a radius-0x7f textured sphere written
   into LIGHT ALONE, centred on Gfx_Rotate2D(-0xc, -0x7e) by
   g_WarpParamA ^ 0x1fff, offset to the frame's 0x50, 0x32. At
   190 fields into the shot the demo's own buffer reads 05 02 29 06
   at the frame's corner - a texel whose colour is 5, 2, 6 and whose
   LIGHT is 0x29, which is what stops it reading as a hole
   [observed].

   0x002d2218 IS A DATA BYTE, NOT SELF-MODIFIED CODE. It sits
   immediately after that routine's rts and is read at 0x002d21dc as
   a longitude bias; Scene_RenderEarth stages it to 0x5a before each
   call. The second bias, subtracted twice, is (0x002e0bec) >> 2 -
   the same yaw the orb's sphere uses.

5.  How It Joins The Shots Either Side

   IT OPENS WHITE AND FADES. Flash_FadeInFromOrbDepth, the last call
   in Scene_RenderEarth [verified, 0x002cdb70]:

       amt = g_OrbDepth - 0x140
       if amt >= 0: nothing
       every pixel: LIGHT = (-amt) >> 1      ; SET, not added

   From the 0x96 seed that is 85 of LIGHT on the first frame, down to
   zero at depth 0x140 - SEVENTEEN TICKS.

   THAT IS THE OTHER HALF OF THE RELEASE'S ENDING, and it is the same
   trick read backwards: Flash_SetLightFromOrbDepth closes the release
   on the same global falling, and Space_RunRelease then calls
   Space_FillWhite for a hard fill. So the two scenes meet AT white -
   neither contains a whole transition, which is the pattern the
   explosion and the release already set (effects/explosion.txt 4).

   THE OTHER END IS NOT A FLASH. The shot closes on the display
   window squeezing shut, section 4 - nothing writes white into the
   frame for it.

5.1.  The Black After It Is A File Load

   WHAT HOLDS THE SCREEN BLACK IS NEITHER A CUE NOR A COUNTER. The
   earth shot is the LAST thing in act one: Space_RunEarth returns
   the moment g_Ham8ScrollY reaches 0x64, SpaceModule_Part0 patches
   the copper with the window already shut and tears the module
   down, Demo_RunAct1 frees its chunk list through 0x0021f93e, and
   Demo_RunAct2 then reads act two's off relic.dat [verified,
   0x0021f82a].

   That read is real file I/O. 0x0021fab0 matches a 16-byte name
   against the chunk table at 0x2c797c, Seeks (dos.library -66),
   AllocMems (exec -186) and Reads (dos.library -42) - and flashes
   COLOR00 red until the left mouse button if the allocation fails.

   SO THE LENGTH IS I/O BOUND, not authored. Measured on hardware
   [observed]:

       +--------+------------------------------------------------+
       | field  | what happens                                   |
       +========+================================================+
       |   21   | g_Ham8ScrollY seeded to 2, the window starts   |
       |   70   | it reaches 0x64 - shut, and the shot returns   |
       |  265   | g_SandCurtain cleared: act two is up           |
       |  270   | the curtain starts opening, +1 a field         |
       +--------+------------------------------------------------+

   195 fields of free and load, then five of the sand scene's own
   setup: 200 from shut to opening, four seconds at 50 Hz.

   THE REPLAYER PLAYS STRAIGHT THROUGH IT. P61 runs off a CIA
   interrupt, not the main loop, so the module does not pause for
   the load and the next scene's music position is set by how long
   this black lasts. A port that shortens it desynchronises
   everything after it.

5.2.  What A Music Cue Actually Is

   Music_ArmCue 0x0021ff3c takes a SONG POSITION in D0 and a ROW in
   D1, stores them at 0x002217bc and 0x002217be and clears the flag
   at 0x002217c0. The replayer compares them once a row [verified,
   0x00220dec]:

       if armed_pos <= (0x0021ffa2) and armed_row <= (0x0021ffa6):
           (0x002217c0) = 1

   so 0x0021ffa2 and 0x0021ffa6 are the player's live position and
   row, and the test is a floor on each rather than an equality.

   Music_PollCue 0x0021ff64 reads the flag and clears it - OR
   returns true outright while the left mouse button is held, which
   is how the demo is skipped through.

   A CUE IS THEREFORE A PAIR OF COUNTERS, not a pattern parse. A
   reconstruction with no replayer can stand one in with a row
   counter at the module's tick rate; what it cannot do is know the
   module's own speed and pattern lengths without reading it.

6.  The Port

   src/part_earth.c is part 2.12b, and src/orb.c holds the sphere,
   the crack network and the affine fill that it shares with the
   release - the two scenes differ only in the parameters they hand
   in, so the machinery is not duplicated.

   THE PLANET IS BUILT FROM OUR OWN SKY. Space_SkyTexture and
   Space_SkyDir expose the two tables space.c already generates, and
   earth_build runs the demo's three passes over them. Nothing is
   read from a capture.

   TWO DIVERGENCES, BOTH DELIBERATE:

   -  CRACK_DRAWMIDPOINTGLOW IS NOT DRAWN, so the blobs it would drop
      on each surviving edge are missing from this scene. It is the
      only place in the demo where that routine is live.

   -  THE CUE IS A CONSTANT. EA_PH2_AT stands in for
      Music_PollCue at 323, measured in a clean run; a part cannot
      poll a replayer that is not there. It is the ONLY thing in
      this shot that music decides - the close and the black after
      it are not cue driven, section 5.1.

   THE SHOT IS 592 TICKS, NOT 591. The demo's loop ends when
   g_Ham8ScrollY REACHES 0x64, so the tick that writes 100 is still
   drawn - fifty steps from the seed. Ending a tick early leaves the
   middle two rows of the frame showing and the shutter never reads
   as shut.

   AND IT HOLDS 200 TICKS BLACK after that, section 5.1. The part
   owns the hold because nothing else can: the demo's belongs to an
   act boundary this port has no counterpart for.
