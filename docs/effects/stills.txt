PCDEMOS RELIC                                              Analysis Note


                         THE BAKED STILLS


Abstract

   Act 2's fourth module shows two pre-rendered 24-bit pictures, a
   landscape and a city street, baked into the executable. The
   routines around them carry the name Dunes_, which is wrong.

Table of Contents

   1. Where It Sits
   2. The DBOD Chunk
   3. The Two Pictures
   4. Open questions

1.  Where It Sits

   Demo_RunAct2 runs six modules and this is the fourth [verified,
   0x0021f894]:

       Sand_RunScene          the sand scene
       TempleModule_Run   0   the temple, effects/temple.txt
       SpaceModule_Run    1   the return flight, effects/return.txt
       Stills_RunModule       THIS
       SpaceModule_Run    2
       TempleModule_Run   1   effects/temple.txt 11.9

   Stills_RunModule 0x00313f6c is three calls: a setup,
   Dunes_RunScene 0x00314304, and a teardown.

   THE Dunes_ NAMES ARE A MISNOMER. They were assigned on a guess,
   and the only dune field in the demo is the Sand_ module's, which
   is a different family in the same code region and is already
   transcribed as part_sand.c. Nothing in this module makes a dune.

2.  The DBOD Chunk

   Dunes_LoadDeepImage 0x0031426a scans forward from A0 for the
   longword 'DBOD', skips eight bytes, and reads 16000 pixels of
   THREE BYTES EACH, assembling a longword per pixel.

   So a DBOD is a 160 by 100 TWENTY-FOUR BIT still. The size
   longword that follows the tag reads 0xbb80, which is 160 x 100 x
   3 exactly [verified].

   A THIRD 'DBOD' IN THE IMAGE IS NOT A CHUNK. The one at
   0x0031426c is this routine's own comparison immediate; searching
   the bytes finds it before either real chunk.

   There are none in relic.dat. Both pictures are in the
   executable.

3.  The Two Pictures

   Dunes_RunScene calls the loader twice [verified, 0x0031435c and
   0x003146a4]:

       0x0031678c   a green landscape of rolling hills under a red
                    and grey sky
       0x003261d8   a city street in perspective, skyscrapers either
                    side and a road running to the horizon

   both decoded and viewed [observed]. They are photographs or
   renders, not anything the demo generates.

   The scene builds its HAM8 tables with 0x7f, 0x5f, 0x4f - all
   three above the 0x3f the rest of the corpus uses, which is worth
   understanding before anything is transcribed.

4.  Open questions

   -  WHAT THE SCENE DOES WITH THEM. Dunes_RunScene is 0x57e bytes
      and only its opening is read: the hook, the HAM8 base, two
      loads and a call to Dunes_DrawSprites 0x003148f8. Whether the
      stills are shown plain, scrolled, or used as a backdrop for
      something else is not known.

   -  WHERE IT FALLS IN THE DEMO. The module is reached
      unconditionally from Demo_RunAct2, so it does run, but it has
      not been captured on hardware and has not been identified on
      screen.

   -  WHETHER THE Dunes_ FAMILY SHOULD BE RENAMED WHOLESALE. The
      module entry reads Stills_RunModule and the loader carries a
      plate saying the family is misnamed; the other four routines
      read Dunes_.
