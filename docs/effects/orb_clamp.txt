PCDEMOS RELIC                                              Analysis Note


                 THE CLAWS CLAMP AND THE SCREEN WHITES OUT


Abstract

   The close of the orb sequence: the claws open further than they ever
   have, snap shut on the orb, and the screen goes white into the next
   scene. Anim modes 0xa and 0xb, phases 8 and 9.

   THESE ARE NOT A SEPARATE SHOT. All three of phases 7, 8 and 9 load
   g_pCamPath12 and step the same cursor two a tick, so the camera
   never cuts - the clamp and the flash are events inside the pan that
   effects/orb_underside.txt describes, and src/part_underside.c is
   one part covering all three. Mode 0x13's shot before it is a
   different camera, on g_pCamPath, which is why part 2.10 stays
   separate.

   The effects are the pan's, unchanged; this note is the clamp, the
   flash, and why that pan was cut short.

1.  The Three Phases

   Space_RunPart runs them back to back after phase 7's music cue
   [verified, 0x002cc8ce onward]:

       +-------+------+-----------------+--------------------------+
       | phase | mode | ends on         | renders through          |
       +=======+======+=================+==========================+
       |     8 |  0xa | g_SceneFuseTicks| Scene_RenderFrame        |
       |     9 |  0xb | its own ramp    | maybe_Scene_RenderFlash  |
       |    10 |  0xc | g_SceneFuseTicks| 0x002cd96e               |
       +-------+------+-----------------+--------------------------+

   MODE 0xa IS MODE 0x12 WITH THE CLAWS ADDED. It loads g_pCamPath12
   at 0x002e4830 - THE SAME PATH as the shot before - steps the same
   g_CamCursor12 two a tick, takes the same 0x100 a tick off
   g_FlatScroll, calls the same Camera_TrackTarget, and advances
   g_BoltSeed, g_BoltPhase, g_OrbSpinA and g_OrbSpinB by the same
   increments [verified, 0x002d628c]. So the pan is ONE CONTINUOUS
   MOVEMENT across the cut, not a new shot, and the port seeds its
   cursor where the previous one ends.

   What it adds is one call: Strut_AnimateClaw with D0 = 1.

   Between phases 9 and 10 the scene is rebuilt and Ham8_BuildTables
   is handed (0x5f, 0x5f, 0x7f) [verified, 0x002cc958], a pale
   blue-white, which is the colour the screen holds.

1.1.  Phase Seven's Length Is Its Cue's, Not Its Cursor's

   Neither of the first two phases seeds a fuse, so no length for
   them is in the binary. Phase seven was taken from the camera
   cursor instead - 0x100 at its start against 0x0ccc at the next
   phase, two a tick, which gives 1510 - and that is 116 ticks too
   many.

   MEASURED AS A MUSICAL SPAN IT IS 1394. The phase opens on the
   (0x19,0) poll at 0x002cc82e and closes on the (0x1e,0x34) at
   0x002cc8bc, and our Cmodule1 takes 1394 fields between them
   against the demo's own 1392 - its cue 6 lands on field 6966 and
   its cue 7 on 8358 [observed]. Phase eight measures 285 on both
   sides, which is exactly what it already held.

   THE CURSOR RUNS ON INTO PHASE EIGHT, which is why its range
   overstates the phase: it is not reset at the handover, so the
   0x0ccc read at the next phase's start is 1510 steps from this
   phase's start and not 1510 steps of THIS phase. At 1510 the pan
   was still running when the cue cut it, and the clamp and the
   flash were squeezed into what was left - reconstruction.txt 4.5
   for why a span has to be measured against the music and not
   against a counter's range.


2.  The Clamp

   Strut_AnimateClaw [verified, 0x002d643c] runs in two stages against
   the three joint angles g_ClawJoint0, 1 and 2 at 0x002e0ba6, 0x0baa
   and 0x0bae, with a latch at g_ClawOpening and three velocities at
   g_ClawVel0, 1 and 2.

   STAGE ONE, OPENING, while the latch is clear and joint 0 is above
   -0x1ee:

       joint 0 -= 2      joint 1 -= 4      joint 2 -= 2

   so joint 1 travels at twice the rate of the other two. THE JOINTS
   START THIS PHASE AT ZERO: hardware reads (-336, -672, -336),
   joint 1 exactly twice the others, which is 168 ticks of those rates
   from (0, 0, 0) [observed]. Joint 0 reaches the limit at tick 247.

   THE LATCH, and D0 is what licenses it: mode 0xa passes 1, and a
   caller passing zero would open the claws and never snap them.
   Space_RunPart clears the latch as phase 8 begins [verified,
   0x002cc8d6], so the sequence is armed once per run.

   STAGE TWO, THE SNAP, until joint 0 reaches 0x200:

       joint += velocity        velocity += 2

   per joint, independently, with every velocity starting at zero.
   THAT IS CONSTANT ACCELERATION AND NOT A RAMP - the increments go
   2, 4, 6, 8 - which is what makes it read as a snap rather than a
   close. Joint 0 covers the 1006 units from -0x1ee to 0x200 in about
   31 ticks; the port measures 247 to 279 [observed].

   THE SHUT TEST IS ON JOINT 0 ALONE, so the others stop wherever they
   are when it passes. All three travel the same 1056 units, so joint
   1 - which opened twice as far - ends at 68 rather than closed, and
   the final pose is (562, 68, 562). Past the limit the routine returns
   without touching anything and the pose holds.

   PHASE 8 ENDS ON A MUSIC CUE, NOT ON THE FUSE, and reading the fuse
   as its length is the trap here. Its loop polls 0x0021ff64 and tests
   0x00237232; the fuse's own flag is 0x00237230 and the loop never
   looks at it [verified, 0x002cc900], so mode 0xa's countdown of
   g_SceneFuseTicks raises a flag nobody reads. Phase 6 seeds that fuse
   0x2b8e and phases 6 and 7 consume 1529 of it, leaving 9621 at phase
   8's start [observed] - over three minutes, which is what the phase
   would run for if the cue never arrived.

   MEASURED LENGTH: stepping the mode word on hardware from 187 ticks
   in, it still reads 0xa at 282 and 0xb at 287, so the cut
   lands between [observed]. src/part_underside.c carries 285. THE SNAP
   FINISHES AT ABOUT 280, so the flash follows within a few ticks - the
   clamp and the whiteout are one beat, not two separated by a long
   hold on a shut claw.

   VERIFIED TICK FOR TICK against hardware:

       +-----------------+--------------------+--------------------+
       | phase 8 tick    | demo               | port               |
       +=================+====================+====================+
       |             187 | -374, -748, -374   | -374, -748, -374   |
       |             207 | -414, -828, -414   | -414, -828, -414   |
       |             247 | -494, -988, -494   | -494, -988, -494   |
       |             257 | -404, -898, -404   | -404, -898, -404   |
       +-----------------+--------------------+--------------------+

   THE FIRST STEP IS ON TICK 1, NOT TICK 0. The phase resets all six
   claw variables [verified, 0x002cca5a] and its loop renders before it
   ticks, so the opening reaches -0x1ee on tick 247 and the latch is
   248. Stepping on tick 0 instead puts the whole clamp one tick early,
   and the error only shows in the snap: the demo gains 90 over the ten
   ticks after -0x1ee, velocities 0 to 18, where a tick-0 start gains
   110 [observed]. The joint values alone cannot tell the two apart at
   the moment of the latch - only the slope afterwards can.

3.  The Flash

   Mode 0xb does nothing but ramp one counter [verified, 0x002d6300]:

       g_WhiteoutLevel += 0x14, and the phase ends at 0x8c

   which is SEVEN TICKS. No camera, no clocks, no claw. Mode 0xc then
   carries the same counter on at 1 a tick while the next scene's own
   fuse runs.

   AND THE FLASH IS ONE GIANT GLOW BLOB. Scene_RenderFlash is
   Scene_RenderFrame with a single call appended - instruction for
   instruction the same nine latch copies, sky, object sort, mode 5
   tail and horizon band, then bsr Flash_DrawWhiteout before the rts
   [verified, 0x002cd8fe against 0x002cd892]. That one call is the
   whole difference, and it is:

       Draw_GlowBlob(radius = min(g_WhiteoutLevel, 0x64),
                     x = 0x46, y = 0x37, ramp = g_RampBolt)

   [verified, 0x002d1f26]. Centre (70, 55) of a 160 x 100 frame, just
   right of and above the middle. The ramp is g_RampBolt, the SAME one
   the bolt nodes and the orb's satellites use, so the flash is not a
   special effect at all - it is the scene's own glow primitive asked
   for a radius nothing else ever wants.

   SO THE SCENE IS STILL DRAWN UNDERNEATH. Phase 9 is not a cut to
   white: the ship, the claws and the sky keep rendering while the blob
   grows to swamp them. A reconstruction that replaces the frame with a
   wash gets the last two ticks about right and everything before them
   wrong.

   AND IT WRITES ONLY BYTE +2. Draw_GlowBlob adds into the LIGHT
   channel through g_LightSatLut, so the screen does not go white by
   having white drawn into it: a saturated LIGHT resolves toward the
   scene's light base, and during this phase that base is STILL THE
   ORB'S (0x3f, 0x4f, 0x6f), which is why the flash reads blue-white.
   The (0x5f, 0x5f, 0x7f) handed to Ham8_BuildTables at Space_RunPart
   +0x3c8 arrives only after the phase ends, so part 2.12 never sets
   it.

   MODE 0xc CARRIES THE SAME COUNTER INTO THE NEXT SCENE, where it
   drives a contracting blob at a different centre - see
   effects/explosion.txt 2. The white peak is therefore at the cut
   between the two phases and inside neither.

   THE RADIUS CLAMP IS WHY THE LAST TICKS DO NOTHING. The level ramps
   to 0x8c but the blob takes min(level, 0x64), so the radius saturates
   on tick 5 of the seven. The port's mean luminance over the flash
   runs 155, 173, 372, 656, 654 at ticks 758, 759, 761, 763 and 765
   [observed] - flat across the last two, as the clamp requires.

4.  The Previous Shot Was Cut Short

   PHASE 8 OPENS AT CURSOR 0x0ccc, measured on hardware less the 168
   ticks the reading sat past it [observed]. Phase 7 opens at 0x100
   and the cursor steps
   two a tick, so phase 7 RUNS (0xccc - 0x100) / 2 = 1510 TICKS.

   src/part_underside.c carried 1382, which came from stepping the mode
   word on from a single reading - that measures how far THAT READING
   sat from the cut, not how long the shot is, and the two agree only
   when the reading is the shot's first tick. The port carries 1510.

   The scroll agrees to within eight ticks: 0x3f00 at phase 7's start
   less 0x100 a tick over 1510 ticks is 0x5900, against the 0x6100
   measured at phase 8's start [observed].
